The three questions

Q1Why did yield bounce back to +166 after 07-13's dry wall?

Fresh term supply re-entered the pool — and almost all of it landed in the second half. The search-term table grew 4,453 → 5,086 (+633) over the cycle, mostly from probe promotion and Casey's in-flight Keyword Layer v1 expansion (new verticals + autocomplete-sourced terms), not from frontier discovery. The new ground concentrated in two niches: Real Estate (117) and Health & Wellness Clinics (115) were 63% of all pitchable. Verify held at 45% (166/367). The takeaway is unchanged from 07-09: we are term-supply-limited, not channel-supply-limited — feed the pool and it converts.

Q2Why were the first nine hours near-barren (34 pitchable / 130 passes)?

Because "committed" isn't "deployed." The 07-13 debrief shipped a fix to campaign-loop.sh so hard-walls rest 30 min instead of relaunching in 20s. It was committed at 07:28Z — but a long-running bash loop does not re-read its own script mid-run, so the process that started earlier kept executing the old, dead-back-off code. Result: the exact 168×-style thrash the fix was meant to kill continued — 84 sessions, median 2.6-min relaunch — grinding an exhausted term pool for the whole morning.

At 16:03Z the service happened to restart (loading the fix). The contrast is the proof: after the restart, 14 sessions, median 82.5-min gap, zero fast relaunches — hard-walls now rest and let discovery/probe promotion re-fuel between sessions. Fixed durably this cycle: the loop now re-execs itself when its script changes on disk, so future autopilot-improve commits self-deploy within one iteration instead of waiting for a manual restart.

Q3Where did the wasted spend go, if the burn-ledger says $0?

Into the finder's Anthropic key — invisible to the autopilot ledger. The campaign fires a full claude-sonnet-5 discovery generation on every fade. This cycle, 168 of 212 (79%) of those generated 40 candidates that all deduped to zero against a term table that hadn't grown since the last dry pass — the same prompt over the same table re-proposing the same known terms. The 44 productive passes added only ~109 terms; the other ~524 of the +633 came from probes and Keyword Layer, not from these calls.

Fixed durably this cycle: a per-mode "discovery-dry" guard records the table size at which a mode last went dry and skips the sonnet call until the table actually grows. It's behaviourally identical downstream (a skipped-dry pass writes 0 terms, exactly like the generate-then-"nothing to write" path) and self-re-arms the instant new terms enter.
