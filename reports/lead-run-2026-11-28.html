The three questions

Q1The loss share nearly doubled. Is the leak actually growing?

Yes, by three people, and that is the first real growth in five cycles. The share moved for two reasons at once: the cohort grew (18 recurring plus 3 new, against 17 all recurring) and the denominator shrank (46 driven against 61).

Hold the cohort fixed and the day still got worse. The recurring half alone reads 39.1% of the day against 27.9% yesterday. The three new losses are what makes this morning different from the last four: lost_new read 0 on every one of those, and the two fields that say whether a 0 is exact both pass today, so 3 is a real count and not a truncated list.

What has not changed is that none of the 18 repeats improves by being planned again. Two have deleted YouTube channels, one is unsubscribed at SmartLead, nine have run out of offers. Those conditions are permanent until somebody writes them down or stops planning those people. One of them has now been dropped on ten consecutive snapshot days.

Q2Fifty-six people were approved and the push drove 46. Where did ten go?

They are waiting for Monday, and that is correct. Siege plans twice each morning: a sample run records the people it covers, then the push run rebuilds the plan from scratch so a block added during the morning is honoured. Here the approval covered 56, the re-plan drove 46, and deferred_unapproved is 0, so nobody was trimmed by the approval gate. Ten approved people simply did not make the smaller re-plan.

This matters because a plan row is not an instruction and never has been. Reading those ten as losses is the 10-22 fault, and it has misreported this pipeline twice before. They are excluded from every loss figure in this report by construction.

Q3What took the 21, and what is fixable where?

By cause: 9 ran out of offers, 2 have channels YouTube no longer has, 1 got no clean job title, 1 is unsubscribed at SmartLead, 1 was already in the campaign, 2 came out of a manifest gap, and 2 left no trace at all. Seven push skips cost only one person, because three of the seven were rewritten and sent inside the same run and one was never planned.

The nine offer-exhausted all failed on one precondition, no safe enemy in the research bank, and the per-variant field reads it without opening a bundle file: eight of the ten leads hold a bank with zero enemies in it, and two hold enemies the skill's safety filter ruled out. Those are opposite repairs. An empty bank is a research pass or a decision to stop planning that person. A filtered bank is a question about how strict the enemy bar should be, and that one is Casey's. The cohort grew from seven people to nine this cycle.

The single unsubscribe is the one cause here that got a code fix, below. Dead channels and the offer rotation live in the send path, which this repo does not own.

Fix shipped this cycle

SmartLead had six addresses unsubscribed and we offered them again every sending day

youtube-email-outreach-v1 · 2c02b28

On 2026-11-19 this block shipped the same repair for bounces, and the note it left behind deliberately excluded the unsubscribe counter, on the grounds that "SmartLead's own block list already carries it into the registry." That reasoning was wrong, and the evidence was already in the log.

It does not carry it. automator/scripts/dnc-sync.py refuses on purpose to read an unsubscribe as a reason: its feed_suppressed() calls one "a STATE fact, not a reason," because a stopped sequence is equally what happens when a client is pulled out of a cold campaign. And the only reason it would file, opted_out, blocks nobody under Operation Siege. So nothing anywhere wrote the refusal down.

Measured in automator/logs/siege.log over the 74 days to 2026-11-27: 65 unsubscribe refusals across six distinct addresses, four of them re-offered 13 times each. All six still read email_verification_result = 'valid', review_status = 'approved_hold' and do_not_contact = false. This is the identical blind spot the bounce fix closed, one door along: sync-smartlead-replies.ts is also the only writer of unsubscribe_received, and it also reads only leads SmartLead accepted into a campaign, so a lead refused at the door can never reach it.

This blocks nobody and files nobody as blocked. Casey's policy is that an unsubscribe answers the one offer it answered, and he still emails these people by hand. The fact being recorded is narrower and purely mechanical: SmartLead will refuse that address from this pipeline every time it is offered, so every plan that offers it spends a slot and a written personalised email on a send that cannot happen.

What it writes: unsubscribe_received and unsubscribed_at on the lead row, the column the pipeline already has for this state. And on a rotated address, verified = false plus a one-time note, which is the write that takes effect today, because Siege's load_addresses() reads contact points WHERE verified, so the address leaves the rotation and a person whose only address this was leaves the plan. Two of the six are rotated addresses.

What it deliberately does not write: not email_bounced or email_verification_result, because the address is live and the mailbox answers, and dnc-sync.py would read a bounce and file bounced, which is permanent. Not review_status or outreach_status, because both are true records of what happened and the shelf counts read them. The refusal is matched by address, never by the counter alone, because SmartLead names the unsubscribed leads in its reply and acting on somebody else's row would retire the wrong address.

That leaves the four main addresses still planned until Siege reads the column, which is a one-line AND NOT COALESCE(lc.unsubscribe_received, false) in its load_people(). Siege is automator's and the send path is not this repo's to edit, so that half is named here rather than shipped. Recording the state is what makes the gate possible at all.

Verified: 22 assertions on the new getter, including the live 2026-11-27 reply shape, the address-mismatch case, the empty list SmartLead sends on every other refusal, and the both-counters case; typecheck clean; the full suite 320 tests green. No send, research, filter or rotation behaviour was changed, and the push's own counters are untouched, so this cannot move a person between failed and skipped in tomorrow's reading.
