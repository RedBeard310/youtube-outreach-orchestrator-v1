The three questions

Q1Why was today the biggest day of the entire block era?

One dominant cause: keyword harvest came back. The autocomplete endpoint had been IP-blocked with sustained HTTP 403s since 2026-07-17 — the only mechanism that injects genuinely-new search terms into the table. Every refill path downstream (frontier discovery re-arms only when the table grows; the anti-starvation floor only reactivates never-run terms) was therefore choked at the source. The 07-31 fix — routing the harvest through a US Webshare egress-proxy pool — restored it: at 05:41Z the harvest ran exit-0, cap 200, its first clean run in two weeks. New ground → discovery re-armed → the pool refilled → the finder had real veins to mine.

The yield landed firm-heavy, exactly as designed: of 843 pitchable, Real Estate & Property led with 327, then Health clinics 153, Coaching 133, Legal 36, Relocation 35, Financial 32 — the frontier verticals seeded in prior cycles finally paying out once terms could flow.

Q2If the block broke, why did the morning still flat-line?

Because the fix landed mid-cycle, not at the start. For the first ~14 hours the term pool was still drained from the tail end of the outage: 48 of 136 passes returned 0 and the campaign logged 23 hard_stop back-offs. Each was the same benign shape — the finder aborted on "No active terms to process," two in a row tripped the two-strikes wall, the loop slept 30 min, retried. This is the system working as designed, not thrashing: no quota burned (0 quota stops), no Claude spent, no crash. The 07-13-era 2.5-min thrash-loop is long fixed; the 30-min back-off held all morning.

The one blemish: those 23 stops all logged as an indistinguishable "hard wall (quota/keys/Airtable)" — when the real cause was an empty term pool. Correct behavior, misleading label. Fixed this cycle (see below).

Q3Is this real and durable — or another leak like 07-30 / 07-31?

This one is structurally different. 07-30 (+41) and 07-31 (+72) were the block leaking — the harvest circuit-breaker punching through on a handful of sessions before slamming shut again. Today the harvest ran clean and repeatably, gated only by its normal staleness cadence (the post-05:41Z skipped:true lines are the 4h cooldown, not the 403 block). Two independent proofs it's the block lifting, not leaking: ① the recovery is a sustained ramp across the whole back half (crests of 23/24/26/27), not three isolated bursts; ② only 1 block-observation all cycle vs. the sustained 403 wall of the prior fortnight.

Caveat worth holding: durability rests on the proxy pool staying healthy. If those egress IPs get burned, the harvest 403s return and we're back to term-starvation. The egress pool is now the single point of failure for fresh supply.
