The three questions

Q1Why is this the lowest-volume day of the whole run?

The 07-17 autocomplete IP-block never lifted. Google's public suggest endpoint (suggestqueries.google.com) — the finder's only working term-refuel — kept returning HTTP 403 to the VPS egress IP. With no fresh terms entering the pool, the reservoir read STOCK-UP on all 20 sessions and the finder aborted "No active terms" pass after pass: 37 of 47 passes at 0 fresh pitchable, tallest pass just 5. Yield actually fell below 07-17 (22 vs 71 fresh pitchable; 0.47 vs 1.09 per pass) — a block doesn't heal itself, and each day of it drains the residual term supply a little further. This is an infra fault, not a code bug and not term exhaustion — the channel universe is untouched; the door to it is just locked.

Q2So why is this a good day disguised as a bad one?

Because yesterday's two self-healing fixes did their job under a real, sustained fault. Compare the same block, one day apart:

① The finder's circuit breaker (68f1896) bailed each harvest after 8 consecutive 403s and emitted AUTOCOMPLETE_ENDPOINT_BLOCKED — it fired 10×, and the day's total 403s collapsed from 22,105 → ~90 (~99.6% less wasted request volume). ② The check-in (8d53f3f) recognised the block and skipped the futile harvest kick 6×, logging free autocomplete_blocked observations instead of hammering the endpoint. Net result: $0 Claude spend, 0 fatal signatures, 0 quota stops, 0 crashes, and everything downstream of the block stayed healthy — verify held ~45% (49 of 108 pitchable). The system correctly rested against an unfixable wall instead of thrashing into it. That is the behavior we designed for.

Q3What's still capped, and what's the one thing that lifts it?

Volume is capped at the term engine, and only infra unlocks it. No code change can talk past an IP-level 403 — the remedy is to rotate the VPS egress IP or proxy the autocomplete calls, after which the block clears with zero code changes. That's an operator action, and it's now the #1 lever two days running.

Two durable follow-ons remove the fragility itself: a second independent term source (DataForSEO / Google Ads API) so discovery isn't riding one blockable free endpoint, and the needs_contact recovery engine — 3,208 found-and-scored creators with no verified email are parked there, and mining them sidesteps the term block entirely because it needs no new finding at all.

Today's fix, in one line

The hourly check-in already skipped its harvest kick when the endpoint is blocked — but the always-on campaign loop's own pre-flight harvest did not, so it fired 14× into the live block this cycle (the old mitigation was a blunt "floor the cadence at 1h", which still storms a multi-day block ~24×/day). 4f3a282 gives the campaign the same block-detector the check-in uses: when the finder's AUTOCOMPLETE_ENDPOINT_BLOCKED marker (or a dense 403 run) is present in the newest session logs, the harvest is skipped and logged; it resumes automatically the first clean session after the block lifts. Components A (campaign) and B (check-in) now agree.
