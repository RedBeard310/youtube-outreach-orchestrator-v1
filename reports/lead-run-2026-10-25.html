The three questions

Q1Is the fleet turning around?

It stopped falling, which is not the same as recovering. Inside the cycle the band gate moved 3 mailboxes back to clear and 2 onto watch, and stopped none. That is the first zero-stop morning since 10-20, and the stopped band held at 34 of 58 after climbing on four straight mornings. Clear went 17 to 18, watch 7 to 6.

Keep the scale in mind before reading much into it. One mailbox back is about 15 messages a day. The fleet has lost 16 clear mailboxes in a week, and a health band costs roughly two weeks of warmup to undo, so a single quiet morning is the absence of more damage rather than repair. It is also a zero-send day, and the gate has pulled mailboxes after quiet weekends before, so one more weekday reading is what settles it. Nothing in the five pipeline repos moves this number. The band gate lives in automator and the decision is about deliverability.

Q2Why did the recovery lane suddenly start spending money?

Because it reached the part of its book where it has to find the creator's website from scratch. The collect pass sorts its 2,401 leads into two tiers: tier 0 already carries an external link, tier 1 carries none. It finished tier 0 and crossed into tier 1 at 08:01Z on 10-24. A tier-0 pass of 150 leads spends about 5 web searches. A tier-1 pass of 150 leads spends 150.

Across the cycle that is 440 searches against roughly 20 the day before, which at Brave's $5 per thousand is about $2.20 for the day where the lane normally spends ten cents. The output did not change: 12 contact points, 2 email addresses from 2 people, 1 of them parked.

The reason the money bought nothing is the ownership gate, and it is the same finding as 10-10 and 10-20 in a new place. Brave answered well: of the 440 searches, 358 came back with a real candidate website. The gate then refused 327 of those 358, about 91%, because the page could not be tied to that creator. That gate is deliberate, from the 2026-08-18 audit, and it exists to stop a stranger's email address being filed under one of our leads. So more Brave credit buys more candidates for the gate to refuse. Two of the 440 searches were refused outright on a usage limit, the first refusals in five days, and even those cost nothing real.

This repeats. Tier 1 is the tail of every lap, so roughly every four days the lane will spend about $2 of search credit on this stretch. That is small, and worth knowing before anybody reads a sudden Brave bill as a fault.

Q3Is anything actually broken?

No. Zero fatal error signatures, no halt flag, no escalation, and $0 billed to the Anthropic API. Enrichment did 1 batch of 1 lead because 6,812 of 6,822 parked leads are already enriched, 99.9%, and ten are left in the whole pool. The verify half of the recovery lane reported no_pending_email_points on five of its six runs and ran the sixth on the 2 leads that had new addresses, which is the queue being empty rather than a selector gap; stranded held at 3.

Apify is at its $10 reserve until the allowance refills on 11-01, so it ran nothing, deliberately. The YouTube key pool reads 15 working of 66, which is settled and inert: the quota on those projects was cut, so a midnight reset cannot refill them, and discovery is off by your order anyway. The orchestrator's own send path fired once at 07:20Z and sent nothing, correctly, because it fires leads parked under review_status=approved and the shelf sits under approved_hold, which only Siege sends from.

The thing worth your attention

Over the prior seven days, 680 people got an email and 10 of them had never heard from us.

That is 1.5%, across five sending days and 686 emails. Friday's 9 first touches were the best day in the series by a wide margin and they are still 9. Meanwhile the shelf grew again, to 5,146 creators enriched and never written to, and the number already written to has moved 3 in nine days. Two separate ceilings hold it there: the fleet decides how many emails a day exist at all, and Siege's slot order decides who gets them, and that order has no term for whether a person has heard from us before. There is already a draft plan on disk for Monday, written on Friday evening before the weekend's band moves and certain to be rebuilt on the morning, and it carries 62 slots against 1,725 people waiting, all 62 going to strong7 and none to the 672 waiting whales. Treat it as a weather forecast, not the plan. Monday's real push is what says whether Friday was a turn or a pocket.
