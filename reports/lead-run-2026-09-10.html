The three questions

Q1Did yesterday's send repair actually hold?

Yes, completely. The 07:20Z run pushed all 11 leads to SmartLead in 150 seconds, across six campaigns: tax and finance, real estate under both writing variants, health, coaching, and relocation. Every lead came back sent_to_smartlead. Yesterday the same 11 failed on all 11.

Two things worth noting, because both were fixed yesterday and both behaved. The campaign IDs resolved before composing rather than after, so nothing wrote an email it could not deliver. And the inbox health gate correctly skipped itself at 18.3h since its last run, under the 24-hour rule, instead of blocking the send.

This closes the 09-09 finding. A repo could not read its own config because the loader only looked at the shared key bank and never at the repo's config fragment. That is repaired at the loader, so the whole class is shut rather than one variable.

Q2Why did parking fall from 133 to 44?

Brave Search stopped answering, and nine of the collector's ten methods need it. The recovery lane finds a creator's own website first, then works that site for contact details. No website, no methods. Both BRAVE_SEARCH_API_KEY slots are answering 402 Usage limit exceeded against a $5 monthly spending cap.

The slide is clean and unambiguous. Across the last ten collect passes, the share of leads that produced no website at all ran 9%, 11%, 18%, 17%, 25%, 21%, 33%, 28%, 60%, 56%, and output fell from 885 contact points off 148 of 150 leads to 186 off 40 of 150. That is an 87% fall in the only lane still producing leads.

And the fix shipped yesterday hit its own ceiling. Yesterday's change rewinds the lane's bookmark when it detects a dead search plan, so leads nobody could search are not marked as walked. It fired three times, then hit a cap of three consecutive rewinds, reset its counter and advanced 150 leads blind anyway. Then it started over. The cap was written for a different failure and it was the wrong tool here. See fixes below.

Worth separating: the leads are fine. A pass that resolves no website has not proved the lead is barren, only that nobody looked. That distinction is exactly what turned the 09-02 batch widening into 71 parks instead of 627.

Q3Is the YouTube key pool cut, or just refilling late?

Still not settled, and today did not settle it, though it narrowed things. The morning probe read 15 working, 50 out of quota, 1 suspended of 66. That is the third identical morning, and now the second in a row where nothing of ours spent a single unit overnight. Our own spending is ruled out twice over.

That leaves two explanations, and the reading cannot choose between them: those projects had their quota reduced, or their refill lands well after 07:00Z. Both readings were taken about twenty minutes past the reset, so per the 09-08 rule they are one measurement repeated, not two independent ones.

The discriminating test is cheap and nobody has run it. Probe the pool again six or more hours after the reset on a day when nothing is spending. If it still reads 50 out of quota, the quota was cut and buying more keys from those accounts buys nothing. If it reads 60 working, the refill is simply slow and the morning number was never meaningful. One unit per key, 66 units total. Carried as lever 3.

Not urgent while discovery is off. The lanes still running use one-unit calls and free page reads, not hundred-unit searches. This needs an answer before discovery resumes, not now.

What broke → what's fixed

The recovery lane's rewind budget was spent on a failure it cannot fix

A collect pass fails to do its job in two different ways: the child process gets killed partway, or it runs with website resolution down. Both leave real leads sitting behind a bookmark that already moved past them, so both trigger a rewind. They shared one budget of three consecutive rewinds.

That budget makes sense for a killed child, where a re-run might well succeed and a child that dies every single time must not pin the walk on one batch forever. It is wrong for a dead search plan. Brave's cap is monthly, so a re-run cannot fix it. The result was the worst of both: three wasted re-walks of a batch it could not search, then a blind advance over 150 leads, then the same again. Over 09-09 and 09-10 a full lap closed with its tail walked at a 27% hit rate.

Fixed. The two reasons now carry separate budgets. A killed child keeps its three. A dead search plan gets 48, which is about twelve days at the six-hour collect interval, so it outlasts a monthly cap. While resolution is down the bookmark simply holds, which costs nothing because every lead ahead of it is just as unsearchable as the one under it, and the re-walk doubles as the probe that notices Brave coming back. It is still a finite number rather than "forever", because a detector that misfires must not park the lane with no error.

The alarm for this was calibrated to a total outage and slept through the whole slide

The hourly check-in watches the share of leads that resolve no website and warns above 70%. Every single reading in the slide above sat under that floor, including the two worst passes at 60% and 56%. So the lane's output fell 87% and nothing said a word.

Fixed. The check-in now judges the lane against its own recent history instead of a fixed number: it compares the newest pass's hit rate to the median of the previous passes and warns on a large relative fall. Verified against the real log, it stays silent on every healthy pass and fires on both damaged ones. It also catches a resolution failure that logs no Brave message at all, which the old check could not.

It reports and does not escalate, on purpose. The remedy is a spend decision, and a fix agent must not make one.
