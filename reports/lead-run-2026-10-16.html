The three questions

Q164 yesterday, 160 today. Did the mailboxes recover?

No. The fleet was the same size. The follow-up bill shrank. Both mornings the plan ran against the same ceiling of 870 and almost the same fleet, 43 live mailboxes on Wednesday and 42 on Thursday. What moved is what SmartLead already owed: on Wednesday 562 follow-ups came due and left 67 slots for new people, and on Thursday the queue had 155 unsent first emails and 1,128 mid-sequence messages, which left 160.

And all 160 went. The push ran from 10:24Z to 11:28Z, loaded 160 people, and lost nothing: no SmartLead refusals, no rate-limit failures, and not one draft turned down by the writer's own quality gate. The manifest disagreement that cost 9 emails yesterday and 131 on 10-07 did not fire at all. Every one of the last three cycles lost something at this step. This one did not.

Which means the day was capped by capacity, not by waste. 164 whale-tier people were ready and 160 slots existed, so the fleet was the ceiling and the fleet was used to the last slot. That is the best a sending day can do under the current fleet.

The follow-up arithmetic is structural, not weather: every offer is three emails on days 0, 4 and 10 and SmartLead sends Monday to Thursday, so a big first-touch day mortgages the following week. The 782-email day on 10-13 is what produced Wednesday's 562-message bill, and Thursday is simply further from it.

Q3The health gate reported 4 mailboxes paused for no reason at all. How?

Because the reasons moved house and the instrument was still looking in the old one. The old gate suspended 4 mailboxes at 09:46Z and resumed 4. The split that matters, deliverability against a mailbox simply being too young, read 0 on health and 0 on warmup. A gate cannot pause a mailbox for neither reason.

All four were health pauses. One read 73.4 against a bar of 90. The new band gate takes health-only suspensions out of the old gate's ledger and deletes them there, on purpose, because the old gate would otherwise resume them on its next run. It does that about thirteen hours after the pause, so a debrief reading in the morning finds the names in the history and no reasons anywhere.

It had been failing quietly for weeks and it rewrites the past. Nothing has been added to that ledger since 2026-09-08, while the history records suspensions on six of the last eight days. Yesterday's debrief read 13 of 13 on health and was right at the time. Re-read this morning, the same run reports 0 and 0. An instrument that changes its answer about a past day without saying so is worse than one that admits it does not know.

Fixed today: reasons are now read from both ledgers, newest owner first, and any suspension whose reason is in neither is counted as gate_paused_unexplained, so health plus warmup plus unexplained always adds up to the pull. On this cycle's live data it reports 2 on health and 2 unexplained, which is the honest answer: two of the four mailboxes have since been released by the new gate and their reasons are gone from both files.
