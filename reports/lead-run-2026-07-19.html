The three questions

Q1How did we recover to +129 with the term engine still blocked?

The block only kills one of two refuel lanes. The autocomplete harvest (Google's public suggest endpoint) stayed IP-blocked and skipped 47 of 66 attempts — but the finder's other refuel, the LLM frontier-discovery path (discover-veins.ts), is completely independent of that endpoint. It fired 66× this cycle, writing fresh probe terms each session that the finder mined in the same session. Those probes happened to land in Real Estate and health-clinic veins that converted, and that carried +129 parked — 2.5× yesterday's +51 and the best day since 07-16. This is the "relentless by pivoting" design doing exactly its job: a multi-day infra block became a degrade, not a halt.

Q2If discovery carried the day, why did evaluate-probes promote 0 winners — again?

Because a probe yields leads the instant it's mined, but almost never clears the bar to be kept. Frontier probes pay off in-session (that's the +129), yet nearly all convert below the 3.5% qualified-rate promote threshold, so 0 survived to the persistent "fresh tier" — the whole book of 3,706 probes sits paused, none promoted. The finder is therefore 100% dependent on re-inventing fresh probes every night instead of accumulating proven veins that compound. The 07-15 recalibration (10% → 3.5%) was meant to fix this; the bar is still above what a single-run probe reaches. This is the day's real structural weakness — flagged as lever #3, not fixed here (it's a business-logic tuning call for Casey).

Q3Why does the finder look "dead" (54/68 zero passes, 0 healthy) on a productive day?

Because the finder's per-pass fresh_pitchable counter badly undercounts the truth. It logged 45 fresh pitchable all cycle while Airtable recorded 252 pitchable discovered — a ~5.6× gap (yesterday it was 22 vs 108, ~5×; the ratio is stable). Read literally, the pass telemetry says "dead day"; the ledger says "productive." The gap also drives the fade signal: because <12 fresh_pitchable trips a fade→discover, the miscalibrated counter fires a discover on essentially every pass (66 of 68). Ironically, that over-discovery is what produced the recovery — so it isn't urgent to change — but the number the debrief and the fade logic run on is disconnected from reality.
