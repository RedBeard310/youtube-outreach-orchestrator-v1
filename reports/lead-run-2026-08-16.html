Q3How good is a re-walk, really?

This is the open question from 08-14, the one blocking the auto-relap decision. There is now an answer and it is not the one the tooling reported.

A "lap" is one pass over the whole seed book. Lap 3 ran from 08-14 07:55Z to yesterday evening and was a genuine full re-walk of 8,341 seeds, then a further 758 fresh ones. This morning lap2-progress.ts said this:

lap2: 694 seeds | 479 qualified | 0.690/seed (lap1 0.373 → +85%)

That reads like the strongest lap ever run. It is fiction. The real numbers:

lap3: 9099 seeds | 479 qualified | 0.053/seed (lap 2 0.373 → -86%)
      | 3487 scored | $7.62 ($0.016/lead)

The bug. The sweep keeps one stats object whose fields don't all mean the same thing. qualified, scored and usd accumulate for the life of the file. seeds_done does not: it is set to the length of the processed list after every chunk, and re-opening the book empties that list. So seeds_done silently resets to zero at each lap boundary while everything beside it keeps climbing. Every reader that divided one by the other was reporting all-time results over one lap's seeds.

It showed up in three places at once. The sweep's own summary printed "0.44/seed" for a lap that ran at 0.053. The progress script subtracted the boundary from a counter that had reset, which goes negative for most of a lap — so it printed "no seeds walked yet" and hid the entire lap — then crossed zero near the end and divided by a tiny leftover. And the boundary writer used the same subtraction, so the error was being saved as the next lap's baseline.

What this means for the decision. Per seed, re-walking has collapsed: 0.453 on lap 1, 0.373 on lap 2, 0.053 on lap 3. The "compounding holds at ~80%" finding does not survive a third lap. But per dollar it is still excellent, because the cost scales with channels scored, not seeds walked, and a re-walk mostly finds channels we already have and drops them for free. A lap costs about $7.60 and returns about 480 leads, 1.6¢ each — cheaper than every lane except keyword search, over a stretch of wall-clock when the lane would otherwise be doing nothing at all.

So: yes to auto-relap, but expect ~480 leads a lap, not ~3,400.

youtube-lead-finder-v1 673a8a6 — one commit. Typecheck clean, 193 unit tests pass, both new paths exercised against the live state file. No .env touched.

1. Lap arithmetic knows which counters reset

New src/discovery/graph/lap.ts holds the one rule the three call sites kept getting wrong: because the processed list is cleared at each re-walk, seeds_done is the current lap's seed count and must never have a boundary subtracted from it, while the cumulative fields must. The sweep summary, the progress script and the boundary writer all read it from there now.

The boundary file also records the lap's own seeds, leads, spend and cost-per-lead outright, so no future reader has to know the rule to get the right answer. Verified: the progress script now prints lap3: 9099 seeds | 479 qualified | 0.053/seed (lap 2 0.373 → -86%).

2. A drained book re-opens itself, and stops when it stops paying

The hourly keep-alive used to end at "no fresh seeds, nothing to do" and let the lane die quietly. It now closes the lap and re-opens the whole book for another pass. Three guards, in order: nothing may be left unwalked, the sweep service must be stopped, and the lap that is closing must have cost under 25¢ per qualified lead (RELAP_MAX_USD_PER_LEAD). A twelve-hour cooldown (RELAP_COOLDOWN_HOURS) keeps a fast-finishing lap from shredding the bookkeeping into micro-laps.

The gate is priced in dollars on purpose. Lap 3 read as a disaster per seed and as a bargain per lead, and gating on per-seed yield would have switched off one of the cheapest lead sources we have. Verified both ways: with the ceiling forced to a fraction of a cent it refuses and exits cleanly with a reason, and at the real ceiling it passes with lap 3 at 1.6¢.

What this will cost. Roughly $7.60 of OpenRouter per lap, a lap taking about 35 hours, so on the order of $5 a day while the lane has nothing fresher to walk. That sits inside your standing authorization for this lane. The first auto-relap will fire on the next hourly tick, because the current lap closed and the book is empty as of 07:11Z.
