The three questions

Q1Did the money problem actually get fixed?

Yes, and it is the only thing that got fixed on its own. Checked live against the credits endpoint this morning: $1,910 bought against $1,610.21 used, so $299.79 left. Yesterday the same check read minus nineteen cents. Casey added $300 inside this cycle.

The recovery is visible to the second in the reply log. The refusals ran from 2026-11-12T22:27:55Z to 2026-11-14T00:48:12Z, about twenty-six hours, and 301 of them fell inside this cycle across 53 failed runs. Every one of the sixteen replies it had been refusing got read in a single pass, and the queue is back to 0 and has stayed there.

Nothing was lost. That is what the pause was built to guarantee: replies stay queued and unprocessed while sending is off, so an outage delays reading rather than dropping it. At the measured account rate of about two cents a day the balance is not a concern again soon, though that rate reflects a pipeline with its discovery and enrichment lanes idle, so it will rise the moment either restarts.

Q3Why did 170 emails reach nobody new?

Because the slots are full of repeat offers and the people waiting are in the wrong tier. The day planned 184, approved all 184, and sent 170. Two of those were first touches, 1.2% against 30.6% across the prior week. Both were first_touch_recovered, meaning a push had already credited them on an earlier day, the oldest on 2026-11-05. first_touch_new is 0, so on the honest reading the pipeline reached no new person at all.

The ceiling is slots, not the ramp. 184 mailbox slots against a day cap of 870, with 267 follow-ups already owed and 5 inboxes full. Behind them 1,263 ready people are waiting for a slot: 560 whale, 625 strong-7, 78 other-7. The whale tier ran out of never-emailed people on the 10-13 push, so its re-offer queue takes the slots while the 78 people nobody has ever emailed keep waiting.

14 people got no email, and 13 of them had been lost before. One is on an eleven-day streak. The causes, ranked by the people they really cost: 5 refused with bounce_count=1, 6 absent from the batch's Siege manifest, 3 already unsubscribed, 1 with no clean job title so no draft was written. Two of the recurring group have channels that no longer exist on YouTube. These are not fresh damage. They are the same handful of people being planned, refused and re-counted every sending day, and they take roughly 8% of a 184-slot day. One caution on the arithmetic: lost_history_capped_days is 3, so lost_new: 1 is a lower bound rather than a count.
