The three questions

Q1Why only +78 parked — half of yesterday?

A third of the cycle ran before its term-refuel existed. The Keyword Layer harvest — the durable answer to term-drought (it pulls demand-verified autocomplete tails, unlike the LLM discovery that just reconverges on its own priors) — was only wired into the autonomous loop at 15:06Z (b588572, the cycle's HEAD commit). For the first ~8h (07:00→15:06Z) the loop's only refuel was the LLM discover() fallback, which we already knew from 07-13/07-14 yields ~0 net-new against the saturated term table. Result: 31 consecutive near-dead passes, ~0 pitchable.

The moment the harvest deployed, supply refilled (term table +200 in the next window) and yield returned — a peak recovery burst, then a long modest tail. Net for the cycle: 168 pitchable · 78 verified (46%) · +78 parked. The verify rate held; the loss was entirely upstream, in the barren pre-deploy morning.

Q2Did the 07-14 self-healing fixes hold?

Yes — both, cleanly. ① The discovery-dry guard (finder) suppressed 105 of 116 would-be claude-sonnet-5 discovery calls this cycle — only 11 actually generated, and 9 of those still came back dry. That is the exact 79%-waste (168/212) from 07-14 now largely eliminated. ② The hard-wall back-off + self-reload held: sessions dropped from 98 → 28 and hard-stops now rest properly instead of thrashing (07-14's morning re-ran at 2.6-min intervals; this cycle didn't). 0 fatal signatures · $0 spend · 0 quota stops — the health check fired no fix-agent all cycle.

Q3Now that the harvest is wired, what's the residual drag?

Two things. ① Between-harvest supply dips. The harvest is cadence-gated (every 4h) and fires only at a session's pre-flight; the dead LLM fallback fills the gaps with nothing. So after the recovery burst, yield sagged at 19:00Z and again at 04:00Z — supply draining faster than the next scheduled refill. ② The wall was invisible to the health signal. A dry finder isn't a crash and isn't a broken verify path, so neither checkin.ts detector caught it — the 8h stall was only knowable at this debrief. That gap (flagged 07-13 rec #4, 07-14 lever #4) is fixed this cycle: a term-starvation heartbeat now logs same-hour (see below).

Fix shipped this cycle — term-starvation heartbeat

scripts/autopilot/checkin.ts now inspects the last 6 finder passes each hour. When they collectively yield ≤1 fresh pitchable or nearly all exit nonzero ("No active terms"), it writes a term_starvation heartbeat to a separate logs/autopilot-observations.jsonl — and deliberately stays exit 0. A term-supply wall is not fix-agent-fixable (the remedy is an ICP/term-supply decision, not code), so spending a claude -p agent on it would burn money it can't recover; and keeping it out of the attention file preserves the fix-agent's evidence channel. It's pure observability: the wall now shows up same-hour instead of only at the next debrief. Verified against this cycle's real data — it flags the all-dead morning window and does not flag the healthy peak or the slow-but-alive tail.
