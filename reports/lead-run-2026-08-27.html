The three questions

Q1Yesterday's fix freed up a third of the day. What did that time buy?

Twice as many finder passes, and not one extra lead. The backoff worked exactly as designed: of 580 chances to invent search terms it skipped 545, and of 580 chances to harvest keywords it skipped 576. Finder passes went from 288 to 565, up 96%, in the same wall-clock.

Yield per pass did not move. It was 0.083 fresh good leads per pass yesterday and 0.083 today, to three decimal places. Twice the passes, twice the leads, same rate. So the keyword lane was never short of time. It is short of terms.

The 15 term-invention calls that did run wrote 3 probes between them: thirteen wrote nothing, one wrote one, one wrote two. The reservoir opened STOCK-UP on all 16 sessions and the starvation reading fired 22 times. This lane is mined out, and today's number is the cleanest proof of it we have had: we handed it double the run time and it returned the same rate.

Q2What happens now the biggest lane has finished its seed list?

It already started. Video-graph walked 8,084 seeds this cycle. That is the 6,721 it had banked plus every one of the 1,363 its refill produced, and it ended the day at zero. The arithmetic is not close: it burns about six seeds for every one it makes.

The cost showed up in the same cycle. Per seed it made 0.0193 good leads against 0.0269 yesterday, down 28%, while it spent 30% more seeds. Its hit rate fell from 5.4% to 4.1%. It still made 156 of the day's 215 good leads, so it is carrying the pipeline, just less efficiently than a week ago.

The other two finished lanes show the settled state. Peer-network walked 200 refill seeds for 5 good leads. The recommended-videos feed walked 202 for 7. Both are asking a creator we found this morning to introduce us to people we already have. Yesterday's day-and-a-bit of warning is spent.

Q3The recovery lane made 77 leads on its first night. What has it made since?

Almost nothing, and the reason is a one-line selector. Contact points written: 46 on 08-23, 2 on 08-24, 0 on 08-25, 1 on 08-26, 0 today. Meanwhile the lane dispatched four collection passes a day at 40 leads each. The pool of leads it has never touched sat at 3,315 the whole time and did not move once.

The selector only let a lead leave the queue by producing a contact point. So every lead the collector failed on stayed at the front and came back six hours later. It has been re-running one fixed batch of the 40 oldest leads about sixteen times.

That batch is the worst forty in the pool. 27 of them have no website recorded at all, so 9 of the 11 collection methods skip on sight. Of the 13 that do have a site, the ones checked today have dead DNS, a bot wall, or a redirect to somebody else's domain. Running all eight of a sample produced 0 contact points. Running eight leads from the middle of the same pool produced 7 points from 2 leads. The pool is fine. The queue was stuck.

This is the same mistake as the verify selector on 08-24, one lane over: reading no result yet as not tried yet. Fixed today, described below.
