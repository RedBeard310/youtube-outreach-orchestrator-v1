The three questions

Q1Where did 288 people come from, and why is that a record?

Six paid batches, and they reconcile exactly. The lane scraped 578 already-known channels for the business email YouTube publishes on the channel page, found 404 addresses, ZeroBounce verified them, and flipped 286 into approved_hold for $40.58. The free half of the lane found the other 2. That's 288, which is exactly what the lead table gained. Two independent instruments agreeing is worth noting, because a shelf drop and a park are easy to confuse.

Q2There's $31 of allowance left. Why can't it be spent?

Because the lane has tried everybody. Starting at 20:20Z and on every tick after it, through 22:21Z, 00:20Z, 02:20Z, 04:21Z and 06:20Z, the batch script answered the same sentence: no qualified lead without an email that this lane has not already tried. Six ticks, twelve hours, $31.34 unspent and $21.34 spendable after the reserve.

This flips yesterday's top recommendation on its head, so it's worth being blunt about. Yesterday's debrief said to put $60 on OpenRouter because November's remaining allowance would deliver about 614 more people. That number came from dividing dollars by price. It had no term for whether anybody was left to look up. There isn't. The honest projection is 0 more people from that allowance until new leads arrive.

And the lane isn't dead, just finished. At 08:20Z this morning, twenty minutes after the cycle closed, a batch found 2 new channels and parked both. So it now lives on new arrivals. With channel discovery paused since 2026-09-08, the only thing producing arrivals is the weekly sweep that re-scores channels crossing 3,000 subscribers. That sweep ran inside this cycle, at 06:48Z, and restamped 36,208 rows while the population moved by 42. A trickle, in other words.

One fix shipped for this and it's in the table below: the snapshot now publishes whether the lane stopped for want of candidates or for want of money, because those are opposite decisions and only one of them is answered by buying anything.

Q3Can the pipeline afford what's left, and what happens Monday?

The credit panic is smaller than it looks, and for a slightly uncomfortable reason. OpenRouter is down to $17.96 and the account spent $24.17 over the last 25 hours, which reads as 0.7 days of runway. The hourly alarm that shipped yesterday fired this morning saying 1.1 days, using a burn rate measured a day earlier.

But look at what that $24 bought. The chain enriched 343 leads at $0.064 each, which is about $22 of it. The burn was the record night. It doesn't continue on its own, because the pool is now 99.8% bundled: roughly 15 of the 7,365 parked people still need enriching. At trickle rates, $17.96 buys about 280 more people, and the lane can only supply a handful a day. So the credit lasts weeks rather than hours. Yesterday's $60 ask was sized for leads that aren't coming.

The fleet doubled, which is the biggest unflagged change in the snapshot. InboxKit now reports 118 warmup mailboxes, all 118 of them SmartLead sending accounts, where it reported 58 all last week. 64 mailboxes were handed a band on or after 11-01, and 58 of them landed clear. Mailboxes clear to send went 12 to 67. They aren't brand new either: each has 32 to 34 warmup emails behind it, so they're past the fourteen-day warmup bar that pauses young inboxes.

On paper that's five times the sending capacity, and Monday is the first sending day since two consecutive zero-email weekdays. The thing to watch is the exact fault that cost 10-29. That day every mailbox in the plan came back with no campaign attached, because list_campaigns() returns an empty list for any failed SmartLead read with no retry and no error, and 62 approved people got no email. A mailbox that genuinely has no campaign yet picks up the same reason. Fifty-eight mailboxes nobody has planned with before is the highest-risk case for both shapes at once.

Neither send-path repair has shipped. Checked directly this morning: why_no_email() still joins its fails list with no coercion, which is the TypeError that killed Friday, and list_campaigns() still swallows a failed read. Both live in automator, which this loop may not edit.

Quiet elsewhere. No email sent, correctly, because Sunday isn't a sending day and the snapshot says so instead of inventing a fault. $0 of Anthropic spend, no fatal error signatures, no halt flag. The stranded cohort holds at 4 people marked sent who were never emailed, down from 54 on 10-28. Discovery stayed at zero, which is Casey's standing instruction. The YouTube key pool reads 15 working of 66, expected and irrelevant while discovery is off.
