The three questions

Q1Why did 111 of 250 planned emails not go out?

One cause, and the evidence splits perfectly. SmartLead answered HTTP 200 for every refusal and set exactly one counter: skipped_in_other_campaign_count: 1, on all 106. Checked against our own send history, 103 of the 106 were already sent_to_smartlead and 0 of the 139 accepted had ever been emailed. SmartLead was taking first-touch people and declining repeats.

It was doing that because we asked it to. Siege sends ignore_duplicate_leads_in_other_campaign, whose name reads backwards: true means skip anyone already in another campaign. Siege has asked for true since it shipped, but the live POST dropped the setting until 2026-10-03, so SmartLead used its own default and second offers went out fine through 10-02. The day the setting started arriving, repeat offers stopped. Casey set it to false at 01:16Z today (becf7fd1a), so the next sending day is the test.

Three of the 106 had never been emailed by us and were still refused. That is the only part of today's shortfall still unexplained, and it is 3 leads, so it is a note rather than a hunt.

Q2Why did it take a full cycle to name a reason SmartLead handed us in plain text?

Two layers threw the answer away, one in each repo. SmartLead's reply was complete and readable:

{"ok":true,"upload_count":1,"total_leads":0,"skipped_in_other_campaign_count":1,
 "block_count":0,"duplicate_count":0,"invalid_email_count":0,"invalid_emails":[],
 "already_added_to_campaign":0,"unsubscribed_leads":[],"is_lead_limit_exhausted":false,
 "lead_import_stopped_count":0,"bounce_count":0}

The push matched that reply against a hand-written list of refusal counters, and skipped_in_other_campaign_count was not on it, so all 106 logged "no reason given". The grounded snapshot then trimmed the quoted reply to 120 characters, and the cut landed four characters before the key: the authoritative JSON read ...,"total_leads":0,"skippe. So yesterday's debrief blamed the SmartLead account rate limit, which did not fire once in this cycle, and the real cause went a day unnamed.

Correction to the 10-06 debrief: the 50 silent refusals it folded into the rate-limit story were this, not the rate limit. The 3 outright 429s that day were real and separate, and the retry work shipped for them still stands.

Q3Everything else was quiet. Is that the pause working, or the pause running out of work?

The pause has run out of work. Discovery has been off since 09-08 so every resource could go into enriching the leads we already have. That job is finished: 7,583 of 7,593 bundled, 99.9%, and enrichment ran one lead all cycle because there was nothing else to enrich.

The recovery lane is in the same place. Its free collect pass is on lap 8 of a 2,523-lead book, 99.3% of what it walked was a re-walk, and it produced 11 contact points across 6 people and one email address on one person. That is a picked-over book, not a fault: website resolution was the healthiest it has been, 1 no-site in 150 against 67.5% two days ago, with zero Brave refusals. Stranded held at 3 for the 24th day. The paid Apify lane added nothing because its month is spent: $10 of the $100 allowance is left and all $10 is the reserve, so $0 is spendable until 31 October.

So the parked pool moved 7,592 to 7,593. The machine is healthy and has almost nothing left to chew on.
