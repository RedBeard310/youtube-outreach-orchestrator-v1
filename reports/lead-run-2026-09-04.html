The three questions

Q1Why did the pool gain 71 instead of 627?

The recovery lane's website search stopped working, and it failed in a way that produced no error message anywhere. This is the single most important thing in today's report.

Here is the chain. needs_contact holds creators worth contacting who have no working email address. The recovery lane walks that pen. For each creator it first tries to find their own website, because almost everything else it does needs one: the contact page, the about page, the email in the footer. If it cannot find the site, nine of its ten methods have nothing to work on and skip.

It finds the site using Brave, a paid web search. Both of our Brave keys are on a plan with a $5 monthly ceiling, and both now answer every request with 402 Usage limit exceeded. I checked them directly this morning. That ceiling was reached partway through yesterday, and the effect on the log is exact:

The checking half then starved. It can only check email addresses the collecting half found. Yesterday it ran full 200-lead batches. This cycle it ran batches of 14, 11 and 17, and skipped two scheduled passes outright with no_pending_email_points. Two thirds of a 200-lead batch converts into a parked lead, so the arithmetic lands almost exactly on today's 71.

Why nothing warned. The search code rotates to the next key when it gets a rate limit, which is error 429. Any other error, including the 402 that means the plan is spent, made it give up on the spot and return an empty list, without trying the second key and without printing the warning it already has for exactly this situation. So a dead plan looked identical to a creator who genuinely has no website. That is the third time in three days that a step reported success while doing nothing. It is fixed below.

One caveat worth stating plainly. The 402 is measured, from a live request I made this morning, and the failure rates are counted from the lane's own log. The link between them is inference, but the two curves match pass for pass and the code path that hides a 402 is right there in the file.

Q2Why was there no discovery at all for 17 hours?

The day's YouTube allowance was spent by 13:00Z, and the discovery loop then knocked on that door 32 times instead of waiting for it to open.

Every YouTube key gets a fixed allowance that refills at midnight Pacific and at no other moment. Ours ran out at about 13:00Z, roughly six hours into the cycle, which is unusually early. The first campaign session hit the wall at 13:07Z, and from that minute there were one or two hard walls every single hour through to 06:51Z. Thirty-two in a row, unbroken.

Why it drained so early is the interesting part, and it is not a fault. The video-graph lane sprinted. It walked 8,509 seeds this cycle against 2,176 the day before, nearly four times as many, because it was finishing its book. It got there: 82,442 of 82,442 videos walked, and the log ends with Video sweep COMPLETE. The video backlog is drained. That sprint is what spent everyone's quota by lunchtime. It was worth it, and it does not repeat, because there is nothing left in that book to sprint through.

What was wasted is the retrying. Yesterday's fix taught the two sweep loops to sleep until the midnight-Pacific refill rather than relaunching every fifteen minutes. The campaign loop never got that lesson. It still backs off a flat 30 minutes, so it relaunched 32 times into a door that could not open before 07:00Z, and each relaunch paid for a reservoir check, a keyword harvest and a discovery model call before the finder told it what it already knew. Fixed below, using the marker the sweep loops already write.

The books refill from each other. A lane finds a good creator, and that creator becomes a starting point for the next lane. That circuit is what has carried the last three weeks, and it works as long as at least one lane is producing. Today only one was, and it has half a day of material.

So within about a day, self-directed finding stops, and the pipeline is down to keyword search and the recovery backlog. Neither is small. Keyword search actually had the best hit rate of any lane this cycle, 18 good leads from 199 channels, or 9%, though on a small sample. The recovery backlog still holds 4,344 creators. But both are finite in a way the circuit was not, and nothing in code can conjure new seeds. What refills the books is either more YouTube quota, a wider set of niches to search, or a new way of finding creators altogether.

Recommended next — ranked

  1. Raise the Brave Search spending cap, or add keys. Both keys are stopped at $5 for the month, and that one ceiling is what turned a 627-lead day into a 71-lead day. It is the cheapest thing on this list by a wide margin and it unblocks the pipeline's biggest producer, which still has 4,344 creators to work through. The fixes above make the failure loud, but loud is not the same as working.
  2. Decide how the seed books get refilled. Two of three are empty and the third has about half a day. The lanes have been feeding each other for three weeks and that circuit is now running down. Three levers exist and all three are yours, not code's: more YouTube quota so the surviving lane walks faster, a wider set of niches for keyword search to mine, or a new way of finding creators. Worth noting the video-graph lane finished its book at 2.7 cents per good lead, the cheapest rate we have ever measured, so refilling that one specifically would be money well spent.
  3. Add YouTube keys. The allowance was gone by lunchtime, six hours into the cycle, and that was with one lane sprinting to finish. Quota has been the binding limit on finding for three days running. Today's fix stops the pipeline wasting effort against the wall, but it creates no new quota. Adding keys is one command on the Mac.
  4. Top up OpenRouter within the week. $193.76 left. Today's 6.6 days of runway is flattered by 17 dark hours; on a working day it is closer to three. Everything bills this one account, so an empty balance stops scoring, both sweeps, enrichment and the recovery lane at once, which is what happened on 08-25.
  5. Nothing needed on enrichment. Only 105 of 4,734 parked leads are waiting on a research file, down from 279 yesterday. The queue that looked like a problem two days ago has cleared itself.
