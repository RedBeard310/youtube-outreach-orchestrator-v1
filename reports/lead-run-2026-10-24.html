The three questions

Q1Did the push finally reach anybody new?

Yes, 9 people, and the shelf moved to prove it. Of 148 emails, 9 first touches went to creators who had never heard from us. The prior seven days managed 2 across 611 emails. Five of the nine came off approved_hold, which is the only kind that drains the shelf, and the shelf's own sent counter went 1,663 to 1,666 after sitting at 1,663 for nine straight mornings.

The mechanism is a tier working through its queue, not a setting somebody changed. Siege fills slots by tier, and the plan has been strong7 for three days running: 134 of 149 on 10-21, 118 of 119 on 10-22, 146 of 150 on 10-23. First touches across those three days went 0, 1, 9. The whale tier ran out of never-emailed people back on the 10-13 push, and strong7 had its own repeat-offer backlog to clear first. It has started clearing it. One cycle is a trend worth watching, not a turn worth banking.

Q2Why is the fleet still shrinking?

Because the band gate keeps moving mailboxes down faster than they recover. Inside this cycle it moved 5 to stop and 5 to watch, against 2 back to normal. That leaves 17 normal · 7 watch · 34 stop of 58, the lowest clear count since the gate took over health pauses on 2026-10-15. Two mailboxes did come back, both on bookcontentgetsclients.com.

This is now the binding limit on everything else. The warmup ramp allows 870 emails a day and the live mailboxes would only accept 150, so six sevenths of the day's permitted volume sits behind mailboxes that cannot send. Nothing in the five pipeline repos fixes this. The band gate lives in automator, and a health band is deliverability, which costs about two weeks of warmup to undo once it is lost.

One reading needs care. At plan time the fleet looked better than the day before, 23 live inboxes and 345 messages a day against 19 and 285, and both readings are 24.0h apart and comparable. The band gate's worse reading was taken at 07:05Z today, after this cycle's push ran at 10:37Z yesterday. So the push got a bigger fleet than the previous day, sent 148, and then five more mailboxes were stopped. Tomorrow's plan is the one that pays for it. A large pull sitting beside a large push is a question worth asking the same morning, not a proof that one caused the other.

The thing worth your attention

The shelf is 5,145 people deep and the fleet can carry 150 a day.

Discovery is paused by your order, so nothing new arrives: the cycle parked 1 person and the pool went 6,820 to 6,821. That is fine while there is a shelf, and there is: 5,145 creators written to nobody, which is roughly 35 weekday pushes at the current size. The problem is the other end. 1,723 people are queued for 150 slots, and the slot count is set by how many mailboxes the band gate will let send. Every mailbox it stops makes the queue longer and the shelf older, and no amount of enrichment or recovery changes that number. The constraint moved from finding people to being able to email them.
