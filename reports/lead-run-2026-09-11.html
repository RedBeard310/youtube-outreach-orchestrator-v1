The three questions

Q1Why did parking fall from 44 to 8?

Because three of the four collect passes were handed a batch that was already known to be barren. The lane keeps a bookmark in the pool of parked leads and works forward from it. Yesterday's change told it to put the bookmark back whenever a pass ran with search dead, on the reasoning that those leads were never really looked at. Sound in itself. But the pass it chose to put back was the one that closed the lap: it came up short, 111 leads instead of a full 150, because it had reached the end of the book.

A short batch strands nobody. The bookmark is cleared, not stepped over, so the next pass starts a new lap from the top and picks those leads up anyway. Rewinding it instead re-selects the same shrinking tail forever. Of the 111 leads in that pass, 53 produced a contact point and dropped out of the queue, which is why the next three passes each read 58 and each returned nothing.

The budget made it much worse. Yesterday's fix gave the search-dead case 48 rewinds, about twelve days at the six-hour cadence, deliberately so that it would outlast Brave's monthly cap. Applied to a lap-closing tail, that is twelve days of reading 58 leads while 3,737 wait.

Q2Is the recovery lane blocked by Brave, or not?

Less than we have been saying, and this is the most useful thing in today's logs. The reasoning written into the code yesterday was that while search is down, every lead ahead of the bookmark is as unsearchable as the one under it, so holding position costs nothing. That is half wrong. Nine of the collector's ten methods need the creator's website, but Brave is not the only way to get one: there is a free route that reads the channel's own page, and it costs nothing and does not go through Brave at all.

Measured through this outage, with every Brave key refusing: the lane still collected from 148 of 150, 143 of 150, 40 of 150 and 53 of 111 leads. So holding position does not cost nothing. It costs the rest of the book, for a cap that does not reset until 1 October.

Both halves are now in the rule. A pass that closes a lap is never rewound. A search-dead pass that still collected from 10% or more of its batch counts as real work and moves on. A pass that truly collects nothing still rewinds, which is the case the rule was written for in the first place.

Q3Is the YouTube key pool cut, or just refilling late?

Still unanswered, and still answerable for 66 quota units. The morning probe read 15 working, 50 out of quota, 1 suspended of 66 for the fourth consecutive day, and for the third straight cycle nothing of ours spent a single unit overnight, so our own spending is ruled out three times over.

But all four readings were taken about twenty minutes after the midnight-PT reset, so per the rule set on 09-08 they remain one measurement repeated. A refill that is consistently late produces exactly this. The discriminator has not been run: one probe six or more hours after the reset, on a quiet day. If it still says 50 are exhausted, the quota was cut and buying more keys from those Google accounts buys nothing. Worth doing before discovery comes back, not after.

What broke → what's fixed

The collect bookmark was rewound onto a lap-closing tail

Three passes, the same 58 leads, zero contact points, while 3,737 leads waited. Would have run for twelve more days.

Fixed in the orchestrator. A batch that came up short closed the lap and stranded nobody, so it is never rewound. The state file also carried a bookmark already pinned to that tail, so it was cleared by hand and the next pass starts a fresh lap. Verified: a fresh lap selects 150 leads where the pinned tail selected 58. 34 tests pass, 8 of them new.

"Search is dead, so nothing ahead is searchable either" was wrong

The free channel-page route resolves a website with no Brave call. Through this outage the lane still worked 148/150, 143/150, 40/150 and 53/111.

Fixed alongside it. A search-dead pass that still collected from 10% or more of its batch advances instead of holding. The floor sits far below the lane's healthy 85-95%, so only a genuine collapse holds the bookmark.

One invisible byte in a YouTube comment kills a lead permanently

Three of the cycle's enrichment runs failed, all on the same lead, all with invalid byte sequence for encoding "UTF8": 0x00.

Postgres cannot store a NUL byte in text at all, and the enrichment run saves comment bodies word for word. One comment containing one of these kills the whole run. Because the failure repeats exactly, the backfill chain relaunched the lead and burned all three of its allowed attempts in five minutes, dropping it from the pool for good. It also happened on 2026-08-25 to a different lead. Fixed in the enrichment repo: the byte is stripped before the row is written, in both stages that save comments. It is never content, so nothing is lost.

Enrichment idled 23.5 of 24 hours

Seven leads enriched. Not a fault: there was nothing to enrich.

Everything that feeds enrichment sits behind the recovery lane, and the recovery lane spent the day reading the same 58 leads. The 09-08 decision to put all resources into enrichment is currently buying idle time, and it will keep doing so until the lane upstream of it produces again.
