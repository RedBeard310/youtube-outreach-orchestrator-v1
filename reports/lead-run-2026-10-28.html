The thing worth your attention

54 ready, researched, verified people are marked as already contacted and have never been sent an email. No lane in the pipeline will ever write to them.

Loading a lead into SmartLead is not sending them an email. SmartLead accepts the lead, then its own scheduler sends later, Monday to Thursday. If the lead is withdrawn from the campaign before that happens, no email ever goes out. Siege does exactly that when a sequence has to be pulled, and it correctly deletes the lead's row from the send ledger, because nothing was sent. What it does not do is undo the lead's own two marks: outreach_status = sent_to_smartlead and a last_contacted_at timestamp.

Those two marks are what every other part of the pipeline reads. So the person becomes invisible three times over. The shelf's inventory count only lists leads at ready_data_scraped, so they drop out of it. npm run send selects the same statuses, so it never offers them. And Siege's own history loader counts a mark plus a timestamp as a send that happened, so its planner believes the offer was already used. None of the 54 has appeared in a send plan since 10-13.

All 54 have a verified email address, a written subject line, a finished research bundle and no do-not-contact flag. 53 of them date from 2026-10-13, the record 782-email day, whose live sequences were pulled that same afternoon when the ICP audit ran. The 782 figure every debrief since has quoted as the record push put email in front of at most 728 people.

The giveaway was sitting in the published snapshot for two mornings and nobody read it, including me. Six people were emailed for the first time this cycle, all six off the shelf, and the shelf's count of people already written to printed 1,667 against 1,667. A running total of people who have been written to cannot stay flat while six people join it. It stayed flat because those six had been marked before they were ever sent anything, which is the same defect seen from the other end. That count is now grounded on the send ledger instead of the mark, and the gap between the two is published with the lead ids, so a stranded group cannot hide inside a healthy-looking number again.
