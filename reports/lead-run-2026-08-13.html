The rows themselves reconcile, which is the reassuring part. Postgres holds 53,913 lead rows, every one a distinct channel, going back to 2026-05-13. I could not separate those two from here, and it is not the sort of thing to guess at. There is no spelling variant hiding the difference either: approved_hold appears exactly once in the new store, at 2,113.

The check that would settle it cannot run from this machine. The migration plan says to keep Airtable in place, read-only, for a week as the rollback path, and pipeline-db/scripts/verify-against-airtable.ts exists to run exactly this comparison. The Airtable token in the shared env now answers 401 AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED on that base, so neither the parity check nor the rollback is reachable. Worth knowing before the week is out.

I did not stop the pipeline over this. The campaign only adds rows, so leaving it running does not make the question harder to answer.

Q3Is the keyword engine recovering?

No. It is the same wall as the last three days, and it is now a small share of the pipeline. Eight of fifteen sessions stopped because they ran out of search terms, the finder logged 10 starvation observations, and 118 of 173 passes returned nothing. Per-pass yield fell to 0.44 pitchable from 0.96 yesterday.

The keyword harvest, which is what refills the term pool, ran 9 times out of 179 chances and wrote 137 new probe terms. The other 170 attempts were skipped: 76 under a six-hour backoff that trips when three harvests in a row come back nearly empty, and 94 under the four-hour cadence gate. Both gates are behaving as designed. The question is whether a six-hour backoff is the right setting on a day when eight sessions stopped for want of terms, and that is a tuning decision worth making on purpose rather than in an unattended cycle.

The niche mix reopened, and that is the sweeps' doing, not the keyword engine's. Yesterday Legal Services was 59% of everything worth contacting, the narrowest mix on record. Today Legal Services is 7 of 391, and the top of the list is Coaching & Consulting (123), Transformation & Performance Coaching (63) and Real Estate (48). Nothing was fixed about term supply. The other four methods simply came back, and yesterday's narrow mix turns out to have been a picture of the keyword engine alone.

youtube-outreach-orchestrator-v14fa0f51Sweep productivity now counts a session that is still running, and the cycle window closes at the cycle end. A log is matched to the cycle by whether it overlaps it, using the timestamp in its filename as the start rather than its last write, so a daemon that runs one long session is no longer invisible. Each session's progress is read as its last chunk line minus its own startup line and then summed, clamped at zero so a mid-cycle seed refill cannot push the total negative. A session that printed no progress at all still reports unknown rather than zero, because calling an unknown zero is exactly how a working daemon gets reported as broken.

Typechecked, nine unit cases green in a new debrief-data.selftest.ts (a long unfinished session, a startup-only session, a log with no startup line, a finished sweep, a refill reset, a crash on the first chunk, three filename shapes), and the whole snapshot regenerated against today's real logs before committing: peer-network 0 → 4,610, the feed 423 → 481, comment sweep unknown → 10, podcast still unknown. The window trim compares parsed timestamps rather than strings, because Postgres returns 2026-08-13 07:23:19.265+00 and lexically that sorts before 2026-08-13T07:00:00.000Z, so a string comparison would have kept every row it was meant to drop. No .env file was touched.
