The three questions

Q1Did the fleet really lose eleven clear mailboxes, or is the instrument lying again?

It really lost them, and we can say so for the first time. Three weeks of these reports have had to hedge every fleet number, because the gate that bands mailboxes can only band the ones the warmup service hands it, so a smaller answer sometimes meant a smaller question. On 11-22 that service dropped from 94 mailboxes to 28 and stayed there, and the next morning's report published "40 mailboxes came off suspension" about a fleet where nobody had recovered.

Yesterday's fix added the roster size to both readings being compared. This morning both read 28, so the comparison is between the same fleet on both days, and the collapse from 16 clear to 5 is a real health event. The gate moved 10 mailboxes out of clear inside the cycle, 10 arrived in "watch" and 4 in "stop", and the highest clear count anywhere in the cycle was the 16 it started at. So this is not a revolving door. It fell and it stayed down.

Seven of the held mailboxes are healthy and waiting out a timer, the nearest one four hours from clearing, so some capacity returns for free today. The rest is deliverability, and the suspend-and-resume lever belongs to the send-path repo, not this one.

Q2What lost the eighteen, and why is it the same eighteen every day?

Because none of the eighteen causes is the kind that resolves itself. Five were refused by SmartLead, eleven never reached it at all, and two left no trace. Ranked by the people each really cost:

Eight people ran out of offers. Two no longer have a YouTube channel. One could not be written to because no clean job title exists for them. Three were refused by SmartLead as already unsubscribed. Fourteen of the eighteen carry a named cause, two are genuinely silent, and the whole set was lost on an earlier day.

Fourteen more drafts were skipped at push time, and all fourteen were rewritten and sent in the same run, so they cost nobody. Eight more people were held back by the approval gate for the next day, which is the gate working, not a loss.

The repairs are known and they sit outside this repo. A lead whose channel is gone sets none of the four things the send program filters on, so it takes a slot every morning forever. Three people with a bounce on file will keep being planned. Both need a change where the people are selected.

Q3Of the eight who ran out of offers, how many need research and how many need a decision?

Eight need research and one needs a decision, and until this morning the report could not tell you which. Yesterday's fix made that row name the offer that ran dry and the reason it declined: love-your-channel-short opens by siding with the creator against something they argue with, and it reported no safe enemy in the bank.

That reason went through a condenser that turns digits into the letter N, so all nine leads behind it published as one row reading (N found, all ruled out). The N is the only part that picks the repair, and it hides two opposite situations:

This cycle it was eight of the first kind and one of the second. Across the whole send log it is 83 leads to 16. That split is now published beside the row, which is the fix shipped this cycle.
