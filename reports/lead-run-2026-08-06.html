The three questions

Q1Did the rescue actually recover leads, or just move rows around?

It recovered leads, and they are the bulk of today's number. All 733 stranded rows were rescored, none failed a second time, and the scores came back real: 188 sevens, 99 nines, 47 sixes and 3 eights, so 337 of the 733 were pitchable creators the pipeline had already found and thrown away.

Those 337 went straight into the verify lane, which is why the arithmetic of the day looks odd at first. The campaign resolved 412 pitchable leads through verify this cycle (142 parked, 270 swept to needs_contact) while only finding 143 new ones. The surplus is the rescued cohort clearing. Of today's own fresh discoveries, only 56 reached approved_hold.

One number worth holding onto: the rescued cohort verified at roughly 31% against 42% for leads found today. Older rows are a colder sample. Recovery is still worth doing, it is just not free money.

Q2Strip out the rescue. Is finding actually back to normal?

Back to where it was two days ago, yes. Back to where it was five days ago, no. Per productive pass the finder wrote 7.1 net-new channels today, against 3.1 on the broken day, 6.6 on 08-04 and 7.1 on 08-03. Fresh pitchable per pass was 1.37, against 1.57 on 08-04 and 2.05 on 08-02. So the incident is fully behind us and nothing is limping.

The slower story underneath is a gentle slide. Pitchable per day across the four clean days reads 313, 259, 184, 143. That is about a fifth lost each day, on a term table that is still growing (14,609 to 15,067 terms across the cycle, so the harvest is working). Terms are being added faster than they are converting. Worth watching for two more cycles before treating it as a wall.

The mix stayed where it has been: Real Estate 37, Legal 31, Health clinics 16, Financial planning 10, which is 66% of the day's pitchable in four niches.

Q3Yesterday's rec was "watch the new alarms actually fire." Did they?

One did, within 22 hours, and it was right. At 05:47Z the pitchable_rate_collapse detector logged its first real anomaly: score-6-plus rate had fallen to 7.5% over the last 67 new leads against a 23% trailing baseline, with the scoring failure rate normal, so it correctly ruled out the token budget bug and pointed at the term mix instead.

The hourly fix agent picked it up at 05:52Z and found the cause. Probe terms were winning promotion on samples of one or two channels, so a term that returned 2 candidates and qualified 1 scored a 0.5 qualified rate and outranked terms proven over hundreds of runs. Off-target terms (addiction and rehab intervention, dual diagnosis, aviation licensing, Whop course selling) had climbed into the active pool on pure noise. The fix floors the denominator at 20 channels for both priority scoring and promotion verdicts, and small probes now sit in a "building sample" bucket instead of being judged early.

That is the loop working the way it was designed: a free detector spotted it, a cheap agent diagnosed and shipped it, nothing paged, and it cost $0 on Anthropic. The scoring failure alarm did not fire, correctly, because the rate was 0.14%.
