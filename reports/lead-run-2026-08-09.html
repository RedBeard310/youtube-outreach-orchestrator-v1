The three questions

Q1Did yesterday's fix actually buy anything?

Yes, and it is the cleanest before-and-after of the whole block. The fix taught the term classifier to remember what it had already rejected, so it stops paying to judge the same phrases every session. It worked on the first day: across 14 harvests it was offered 58,746 candidates and skipped 54,129 from cache, which is 92.1%. Only 4,617 phrases got sent to a model, against 38,869 yesterday.

The saved time landed where it was supposed to. Every session opens by topping up its search terms before it can search for anything, and that opening step used to take 74 minutes. Today it took 21. Over the cycle that is 301 minutes of startup instead of 668, and that is with 14 sessions running rather than 9. The campaign's own 90-minute clock never changed. What changed is that a "90-minute session" used to occupy 156 minutes of real time and now occupies about 100, so more of them fit in a day.

Everything downstream moved with it: 120 finder passes against 87, 1,046 new leads against 757, 294 pitchable creators against 246, +93 parked against +70. Density per hour of session time went from 3.5 passes to 5.1. Nothing about the finder itself got better today. It simply got more of the day.

Q2Then why is parking still only +93?

Because email is still the wall, exactly as it was. Of 294 pitchable creators found today, 93 landed in approved_hold and 180 went to needs_contact, the lane for creators worth contacting whose email address nobody could verify. That is a 34% verify rate, in the same band it has held all month. More intake gives you more of both piles in the same proportion.

needs_contact now holds 5,221 and grew by 181 today against the parked pool's 93. Together the two lanes are 9,487 creators found, scored, and never contacted. One genuine improvement worth recording: the niche mix widened. The top three niches supplied 37% of today's pitchable, down from 49% yesterday, and Real Estate stopped dominating on its own (43 of 294, with Coaching 34, Financial Planning 33 and Health clinics 32 close behind).

Q3What did the reclaimed time reveal?

The autocomplete term source is mined out, and the loop had no way to notice. Now that the machine stops re-judging its own rejects, what is left is a clear reading of how much genuinely new material the source still holds. Across 14 harvests it wrote 15 usable terms. Seven of the fourteen wrote nothing at all. Even among the 4,617 phrases that were genuinely unjudged, the keep rate was 0.32%.

The gate that triggers the harvest cannot be satisfied. Every session begins with a stock-up check on the term reservoir. All 14 opened the same way: STOCK-UP, fresh: 0, short_by: 48. That verdict forces a harvest at a one-hour floor, deliberately overriding the normal four-hour cadence. The handful of terms a harvest does write get consumed by the next few passes, so the following session reads fresh: 0 again and fires another one. Fourteen sessions, fourteen forced harvests, fifteen terms.

Term supply is not at risk, which is what makes this pure waste. The pool is being carried by the other two sources: the proven-term revive shipped on 08-07 fired 31 times and the invented-vein discovery ran 133 times with 117 promotions. The result was 0 hard stops and 0 quota stops, and the term pool visibly rebuilt inside every session. The harvest is the third leg, and it is the dry one.

youtube-outreach-orchestrator-v144233a0Low-yield backoff for the keyword harvest. The campaign now reads how many probes a harvest actually wrote and remembers it in logs/keyword-harvest-yield-state.json. When the last three harvests each wrote fewer than three, it skips the harvest for six hours and spends the time on finder passes instead. The check sits before the cadence gate on purpose, because the stock-up pre-flight overrides that gate down to one hour, and that pre-flight is the caller that was force-firing an exhausted source every session. Self-clearing: after the window one harvest always runs, and a single good yield clears the streak and restores normal cadence. That matters because harvest yield is not permanently dead. Editing the prefilter skill or repointing models.json throws away the reject cache and makes everything judgeable again, and new seeds do the same. A harvest whose output can't be read records no sample at all, so an unreadable harvest can never quietly disable itself. Escape hatch HARVEST_LOW_YIELD_BACKOFF_HOURS=0.

Verified before commit: TypeScript clean; 14 behaviour checks green, covering the streak, the window expiry, a good yield clearing a streak mid-run, cold start, the disable switch, and the fail-open path; and the output parser was run against the real harvest text from two live 08-09 sessions and read both correctly. No API calls, so it cost nothing.
