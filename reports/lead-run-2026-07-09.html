01What actually happened

Today was the debut of the autonomous campaign driver we built yesterday. Instead of hand-running finder → verify → promote, one command (npm run campaign) now: checks the term reservoir, finds leads, verifies emails overlapped with the next find pass, invents fresh search terms when a vein fades, promotes verified leads each pass, and sweeps dead-email leads into the recovery lane — all on its own, stopping only at target or a hard wall.

It worked — but the debut was also a shakedown cruise. The morning's first automated run found 219 pitchable leads and then parked zero of them: a path bug meant every verify/promote step crashed. We caught it, fixed it, proved the fix, and the rest of the day the machine grond steadily from ~160 to 480. The value of a first supervised run, exactly.

02What worked · what didn't

Worked well

  • The autonomous loop, end-to-end. After the fix, 5 cycles ran hands-off — find, verify, promote, sweep, self-restock — for hours.
  • Per-pass promote made the parked count grow live and let the run self-stop at target.
  • The discovery engine invented real winners — e.g. how to scale a med spa without an md converted 29%, plus luxury real estate, functional-medicine and sales-team veins.
  • needs_contact auto-sweep captured all 539 dead-email leads — nothing thrown away.
  • Verify rate held steady at ~47% all day — no scraper degradation under load.
  • The time-box margin fix stopped cleanly before every deadline after we added pass-duration reserve.

Didn't work / bit us

  • Path bug (critical). IDs files were passed to cross-repo child processes as relative paths → every verify/promote ENOENT'd → morning run parked 0. fixed
  • llm-cap 800 was a bad trade — 60% longer passes for only ~35% more leads. Reverted to 500. reverted
  • firm-tilt silently capped. Airtable's firstPage() returns ≤100 rows, so the "over-fetch 210 and re-rank" only ever saw the top 100 active terms. open fix
  • Discovery saturation. Late restock calls on mined niches returned 0 net-new — the generator kept re-proposing terms already in the table.
  • Broad/unfocused discovery = 0 yield. Only focused-by-niche calls produced.
  • Ops slip: one relaunch was backgrounded-inside-a-background call and orphaned from tracking (recovered via the monitor).

03What converted · what didn't

Good veins

  • Health & Wellness Clinics — the surprise star. Med-spa / functional-medicine / weight-loss practice-scaling terms hit the day's highest qualified-rates (top: 29%).
  • Sales Training — team-building, luxury, sales-leadership angles; multiple ≥14% winners.
  • Real Estate — luxury agent marketing & raising capital.
  • Legal & Financial — steady, and the best email-verify rates (firms publish contact info).
  • Shape: how to scale a [practice] without [X] — the strongest new pattern discovered today.

Poor veins

  • 95% of generated probes converted <10%. Only 12 of 240 tested probes were clear winners — though the low-rate tail still produced most raw volume.
  • Coaching / Consulting & Practice-Growth restocks returned near-0 net-new — fully mined.
  • Unfocused/broad discovery — 0 useful terms every time.
  • Generic "get clients for [X]" tail — increasingly dominated by low-intent creators with no published email (they land in needs_contact, not approved_hold).

04Are we running dry? — the question that matters most

Short answer: no — but we're mining a small quarry with a big shovel. Channel supply is healthy; novel-term supply is the ceiling we keep hitting. Two independent signals point the same way:

Signal A — every fresh term still finds creators

Each brand-new search term still yields ~0.9 pitchable creators, and that ratio held steady from the first pass to the last. The finder is not struggling to find channels per term. If we had more good terms, we'd find more good creators.

Signal B — but generating new terms is getting hard

The real read: we've deeply mined ~10 niches. That is a tiny slice of the ICP — "any high-ticket, service-based business that can win clients on YouTube." We haven't meaningfully touched: adjacent professions, sub-specialties, geographic long-tail, or entire verticals (aesthetics beyond med-spa, home-services coaching, B2B consulting sub-fields, healthcare private practice, franchise/agency niches, etc.).

So: the pool of good creators is large and nowhere near exhausted — but our current term-generation approach (re-mining the same 10 niches) is hitting diminishing returns. The fix isn't "find more channels," it's "widen the niche frontier the generator explores."

06Fix · optimize · cut

Fix

  • bug firm-tilt firstPage() 100-row cap — page the active-term pool properly.
  • auto run evaluate-probes automatically at each cycle's end (currently manual) so winners get promoted and losers retired without a human.
  • ops a one-line "relaunch next cycle" wrapper so I stop hand-orphaning processes.

Optimize

  • Widen the discovery frontier — pass the generator the list of saturated niches and tell it to explore adjacent/new verticals; this is the single biggest volume lever.
  • Concurrent finder passes to beat the ~45-min wall-clock.
  • Keep llm-cap at 500 — proven best throughput.
  • Bank the needs_contact lane — 2,173 reachable-but-unverified creators; even 40% recovery ≈ ~870 leads with zero new finding.

Cut / remove

  • llm-cap > 500 — worse throughput, no benefit.
  • Unfocused / broad discovery calls — always 0 net-new; only focus-by-niche produces.
  • Re-mining fully-saturated niches (Coaching/Consulting, Practice-Growth) — stop pointing discovery at them until the frontier widens.
  • Legacy bump-new-terms.ts — superseded now that fresh terms insert at priority 75.

07Two things to check

1,821 leads sit at review_status = approved. That's a large pool outside today's approved_hold work — they'd be tick-eligible if the tick were running. Worth a look: are these stuck/unprocessed, or intentionally parked? If genuine, that's another big latent batch.

The needs_contact lane is now the biggest untapped asset in the whole pipeline — 2,173 high-signal creators we found and scored but couldn't auto-email. Building even a modest contact-recovery step (About-tab email, booking links, contact-form scrape) likely out-yields another day of fresh finding.
