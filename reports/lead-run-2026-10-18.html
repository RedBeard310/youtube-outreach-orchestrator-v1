Q2Ten mailboxes came back in one hour. Did they heal, or did the rule change?

Neither. The rule finally had enough history to let them go. Leaving the watch band takes a clean reading plus a score of 90 or better held for 48 hours without a break. All ten were clean the whole time, each at 96.7 or 98.4 with zero spam landings across 39 to 56 warmup emails. But the gate took over on 2026-10-15 at 22:57Z and cannot credit clean time from before its own first run, so all ten carried the same score_clean_since stamp: that first run. Forty-eight hours later the whole cohort aged out together, which is the 23:05Z move.

The cost landed on Friday. Those ten were sitting on follow-ups-only when the push ran, which is why it printed 18 live mailboxes and 82 slots.
