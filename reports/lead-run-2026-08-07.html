The three questions

Q1Parking fell from +173 to +32. Is something broken?

No. It ran out of search terms, and the reserve that was supposed to cover that was already spent. At 13:00Z the hourly agent retired 2,101 active terms after tracing a pitchable-rate collapse to the keyword prefilter: 93% of the active pool sat in five niches and was almost entirely personal-curiosity searches like "cessna 150 cost of ownership" and "yacht running costs", not business-creator content. Retiring them was right. It also took the pool from roughly 2,233 terms to 132.

What should have happened next is the anti-starvation floor built on 2026-07-10 pulling good paused terms back in. It didn't, and it said so plainly: 30 times this cycle the finder logged NO never-run paused terms remain. That floor only ever revives terms that have never been run, and that reserve drains permanently, because every term eventually gets a run. It emptied at some point and quietly stopped being a safety net.

Counted directly against the live term book: 15,397 terms, of which 28 active, 13,251 paused and 2,118 retired. Sitting inside those 13,251 were 3,348 proven converters that had cooled for three weeks or more, including terms measured at 13 to 19% qualified rate whose last run was in July. The finder hard-stopped seven times while that was on the shelf.

Q2Was throwing away 2,101 terms actually the right call?

Yes, and the numbers back it. On the terms that survived, the pitchable rate held at 19.3% (110 of 571) against 20.1% yesterday, and net-new channels per productive pass went up, 7.1 to 8.6. Cutting a third of the pool cost volume and cost nothing in quality, which is what you'd expect if the cut terms were genuinely junk.

The agent fired a second time at 00:03Z on a recurrence with a different cause, and that one is worth knowing about. The keyword harvest only deduplicates on the exact string, so autocomplete's expansion of one popular branch, "solicitor for defamation of character", exploded into about 32 respellings of the same query: "lawyer for defamation lawsuit", "civil attorney for defamation of character", and so on, most returning under 7 channels each. By 23:47Z 12 of the pool's 18 active terms were respellings of one branch. The fix collapses role-word synonyms to a single phrase signature and caps how many variants of one shape get written. It had happened on four separate days before anyone noticed.

The niche mix stayed where it lives: Legal 22, Health clinics 14, Manufacturing 10, Real Estate 9, which is 50% of the day's pitchable in four niches.

Q3Yesterday's rec was "confirm the auto-rescue fires when something is actually stranded." Did it?

The trigger works. The recovery is still untested, because nothing got stranded. The auto-rescue shipped yesterday evaluated itself 12 times. It ran on 5 of them and exited clean each time with a measured scoring failure rate of 0%. It correctly declined the other 7, all with insufficient_sample, because those sessions had produced too few new leads to prove the scorer was healthy: 0, 0, 0, 5, 17 and 24 new leads against a threshold of 25.

Two of those refusals sat at 17 and 24, just under the bar, on sessions that were short because the term pool was dry. That is the guard doing its job rather than a tuning problem: a session that barely ran is exactly the session whose scoring health you shouldn't trust.

Scoring itself stayed perfect for a second day: 0 failures, 0.0%, no stranded rows, no fatal signatures for the eighth straight cycle, and $0 on Anthropic.
