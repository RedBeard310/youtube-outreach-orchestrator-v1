The three questions

Q1175 emails, nothing failed. Good day?

A clean push, and a day that reached nobody new. Those are two different things, and for five mornings in a row they have printed the same numbers.

169 people got an email. All 169 had been emailed before. Over the prior seven days, 882 of 1,153 people were first touches, so 76.5% of that week was new people and 0% of yesterday was. The shelf gives the same answer from the other side: of the 6,817 people parked and ready, 5,144 have never been written to and 1,663 have, and that second figure has been 1,663 every morning since 10-16 while hundreds of emails went out. Sending does not move a lead out of approved_hold, so a repeat offer leaves both numbers exactly where they were.

It is a queue, not a fault. Siege fills mailbox slots in tier order, then score, then subscriber count, and nothing in that sort key asks whether a person has heard from us. The whale tier ran out of never-emailed people on the 776-person push of 10-13, so its re-offer queue has first call on every slot. Yesterday 443 whale-tier people were waiting, 176 slots existed, and the 813 people in other7 and the 171 in strong7 got none of them.

Repeat offers are deliberate, your call on 10-07. The problem is that the shelf is the only inventory the business has, it holds five thousand people, and nothing currently drains it. At the pace of the last five sending days, the never-written-to half of the shelf is not being worked at all.
