The three questions

Q1Was this actually a good day — and why did the auto-debrief nearly say it wasn't?

Yes, genuinely good. 409 pitchable found, 162 verified and parked into approved_hold (pool 1,770 → 1,826), 201 more swept to needs_contact, all for $0 of Claude spend. But the raw grounded snapshot the debrief agent was handed read 1 pitchable · 4 finder runs · +0 parked — a near-dead day.

Root cause: an instrumentation off-by-one, not a pipeline failure. debrief-data.ts computed the cycle window as [this-morning's PT-midnight → now]. The debrief timer fires at 00:20 PT — so that window was only the 20 minutes since midnight, capturing the tail 4 finder passes instead of the full 24h that just ended. Every autopilot debrief would have shipped near-empty. Fixed: the window is now the trailing 24h ending at the most-recent PT midnight (cycle_end_iso added for transparency); re-running the gatherer produced the corrected figures this report is built on.

Q2Did the 07-10 anti-starvation fixes actually hold?

They held. 07-10's signature failure was the active term pool decaying entirely to negative priority → 17 consecutive 0-yield passes re-mining saturated ground. Today: 35 zero passes out of 131 — but scattered, interleaved with positive passes, never a contiguous stall. The anti-starvation floor (auto-reactivate untapped paused terms), fast dead-term pause, and mid-run probe promotion kept fresh minable supply flowing. The day never ground; it trickled. Frontier discovery ran 111 cycles and promoted every time, feeding the active pool continuously (pool size climbed 40 → 188 over the session).

Q3What's the ceiling now?

Two throttles, both known and both structural — not bugs:

① Term-supply, still. Frontier passes yield a consistent ~3.0 fresh pitchable each (398 / 131) — reliable but flat. There's no big vein; growth comes from widening the frontier, not grinding harder. ② Email verify at ~40%. Of 409 pitchable, only 162 had a deliverable email → parked; 201 went to needs_contact (found, scored ≥6, no verifiable email). That lane is now 2,557 creators deep and remains the single largest unbuilt lever in the whole system.
