The three questions

Q1Why did the day park almost nothing (+1)?

Two independent supply engines emptied on the same day. For days 2–8 of the block, finding was dead but parking kept rising (+151 → +86 → +67 → +7) because the verify lane was draining a standing backlog of found-but-unverified creators into approved_hold. That backlog is now spent. So today both taps are dry at once: nothing new is found, and there's nothing left in the pool to verify and promote. The single +1 is one Real Estate creator that happened to surface + verify — statistical noise, not a working pipeline.

Where the drained backlog went: needs_contact now holds 3,683 found-and-scored creators with no auto-verifiable email. That lane has been the pressure-relief valve all week — but it's a parking lot, not a driver of sends, and it's inert until the recovery engine is built.

Q2Why did fresh finding go from 52 net-new (Day 8) to 3 (Day 9)?

Same block, but the reservoir finally hit true zero. On Day 8 the endpoint was rate-limiting, not banning — a harvest could still leak ~8 seeds / ~890 terms through before the breaker tripped, so a trickle of channels got written. Today the harvest fired 3× and again leaked terms (862 net-new across 8 seeds at the 02:17Z run) — but the ICP prefilter kept 0 of 862. Every term that leaks now is a saturated vein we've already mined.

Downstream, that leaves the finder with an empty active pool AND no never-run paused terms to fall back on — the anti-starvation floor (built 07-10) has nothing left to reactivate: "active pool exhausted and NO never-run paused terms remain". So the finder aborts in 0.8s with zero searches spent. That's why burn and quota are both untouched — it's not grinding, it's resting.

Q3Is anything actually broken — or is this the machine working?

Nothing is broken. This is the self-healing design doing exactly what it should. Reservoir read STOCK-UP → harvest attempted → block-backoff correctly skipped it 46× and probed 3× (the 6h `1d5d1f9` backoff, holding) → finder found no terms → two clean aborts tripped the benign hard-wall → the loop rested 30 min and tried again. 45 times. $0 Claude, 0 crashes, 0 quota stops.
