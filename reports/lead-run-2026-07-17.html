The three questions

Q1Why was this the lowest-volume day of the run?

The term pool was starved — but this time by a network block, not by exhaustion. The finder mines active search terms; when the pool runs thin, the keyword harvest is the only thing that refills it (free autocomplete tails → ICP-prefiltered → probes). This cycle the harvest was wholesale IP-blocked: Google's suggest endpoint returned HTTP 403 to both the youtube and google autocomplete engines, 22,105 times. So the harvest fired but produced ~nothing, the reservoir gate read STOCK-UP on all 29 sessions, and 27 of 28 sessions hard-stopped on the term wall. Result: 71 fresh pitchable across 65 passes (1.09/pass) — the fewest passes and the lowest raw total of the whole run.

The tell that this is upstream of everything that works: verify held at ~47% (75 verified of 159 pitchable), right on trend. Nothing downstream broke. The loss was entirely in finding, and finding was choked at its supply valve.

Q2We shipped the harvest to fix exactly this — why didn't it save the day?

Two reasons stacked. (1) It was blocked at the source. The 07-14/07-16 work wired the harvest into the loop and added fade-firing — but a harvest can only refuel if the endpoint answers, and this cycle it answered 403. (2) It was cadence-gated too tight. Of 66 harvest attempts, only 6 fired — 60 were skipped by the interval gate. A mid-cycle fix (the STOCK-UP-triggered 1h force-harvest, 0df4923) landed at 01:50Z and was only live for the final ~5h. But note: even the 6 that did fire hit the 403 wall — so cadence was the lesser problem today. The block was the wall.

A subtle aggravator we also fixed: the check-in's starvation backstop kicks a keyword harvest to break a dry spell — which, into a blocked endpoint, just hammers the block harder and deepens it.

Q3Is the term well finally dry, or is this a block?

A block — categorically, not exhaustion. Three proofs: ① The failures are HTTP 403 (access denied), not empty responses — the endpoint is refusing us, not out of suggestions. The harvest even has a separate "0 net-new = throttling" guard for the exhaustion case; that's not what fired. ② Both the YouTube and Google suggest corpora 403 in lockstep — that's an IP-reputation block on the shared suggestqueries.google.com host, not two independent term veins drying up on the same day. ③ Everything that doesn't depend on that endpoint stayed healthy — 883 channels discovered, 159 pitchable, verify ~47%, 0 quota stops. When the block lifts (or we change egress IP), the refill resumes with zero code changes.
