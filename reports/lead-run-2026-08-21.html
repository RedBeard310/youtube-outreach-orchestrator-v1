The three questions

Q1Did yesterday's refill fix hold?

Yes, on every number it was measured against. At 20:30Z the refill fired for the first time in its life and logged merged +3,180 new seeds, 12,044 upgraded (19,898 total, 3,218 remaining). The lane then walked 11,455 seeds, double the 5,718 it managed yesterday, and delivered 3,133 channels and 201 good leads.

The merge behaved as designed too. It kept the walked-seed list and the lifetime stats instead of wiping them, so none of that 11,455 was re-walking ground the lane had already covered.

Q2Then why did the day halve?

Because the seed supply behind all of it is smaller than the walking. Three lanes hit the end of their books inside one cycle.

The recommended-videos feed, the lane the standing orders name primary, is at 10,555 of 10,555 walked. Its hourly refill is working perfectly and is finding seven to ten new seeds an hour, because its seed source is our own newly qualified leads and we qualify about 265 a day. It restarted 25 times today to walk 257 seeds. Yesterday it walked about 10,300.

The peer-network lane is in the same state: 10,444 of 10,444 walked, 262 seeds advanced, 7 leads.

And the video-graph sweep, the one that carried the day, ended the cycle with 417 seeds against a demonstrated 11,455 a day. Its refill query returned 15,410 seeds in total, so tomorrow's tick would have added only the few hundred videos belonging to channels qualified today. It was about 24 hours from becoming the same kind of trickle.

None of this is a fault to repair. Every service ran, every timer fired, every refill did what it was written to do. The pipeline is now limited by how much unwalked material exists, not by uptime, quota or code.

Q3Was anything watching for this?

No. The health monitor reported all three drained lanes as productive: true. It asks two questions of a lane, is it alive and did it advance any seeds, and 257 seeds is more than zero.

That is the gap that let a 92% collapse pass without a single warning. A lane whose book is finished looks identical to a healthy one until the next morning's lead count arrives, by which point the day is already spent. The monitor was built two days ago to catch exactly this class of silent stall, and it could not see this shape of it.

Fixed today. Every sweep state file already carries its seed list and its walked list, so the road left is free to compute. Lane health now reports it, in the unit that matters.

  2. The lane monitor cannot see an empty book. Q3. Fixed at the source rather than patched in the reader, and the new fields flag two lanes on the live state immediately.

Lap 4 to lap 5 held 58%, not 80%, and lap 5 is running at 4% of the first lap. Re-walking that book is no longer worth the walk, which is a finding about the lane rather than a bug in it.

Not a fault: the keyword lane's 21 term-starvation heartbeats and 3 hard stops, which is its documented state since it was demoted to opportunistic on 08-14; 11 sessions ending on their time budget; the comment sweep contributing 0 leads from 159 channels on a run that fired at 09:11Z, before your pause took effect (its timer is confirmed disabled and inactive now); $0 Anthropic for the twenty-second cycle; zero crashes, quota stops, scoring failures and fatal signatures.
