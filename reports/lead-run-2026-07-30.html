The three questions

Q1Why did a six-day flat streak suddenly produce +41?

The block eased from hard-wall back toward rate-limiting — the 07-24 pattern, larger. The autocomplete endpoint is still IP-blocked (Day 14), so the harvest still can't refill cleanly, but the circuit breaker got ~8 of 42 seeds through before tripping in two separate windows. Those partial refills gave the finder real terms to mine: it wrote 592 net-new channels across 60 passes-with-writes, 111 cleared the ≥6 bar, 41 had a verifiable email → +41 parked.

Crucially this is genuine finding, not backlog churn: the found-but-unverified backlog that carried days 2–8 of the block was spent on 07-25, so fresh_finding_dead=false and fresh_pitchable_sum=110 can only mean the finder actually surfaced new creators. Niche mix is the classic frontier trio — Health clinics 39 · Real Estate 31 · Financial Planning 19 = 80% of pitchable. It's not a machine change and not a fix; it's which creators the throttle let through today. It could narrow again tomorrow.

Q2Then why was the whole first half of the day dead?

Because the block is rate-limiting, not a steady leak — the breaker only wins a seed-batch when its request window happens to open. From midnight to noon PT the term engine had nothing: the active pool drained overnight with NO never-run paused terms left to reactivate (the 07-10 anti-starvation floor is empty), and discovery is dry-locked (went dry at 7283 terms — no refuel while blocked). So the finder aborted No active terms in ~0.8s on 22 straight single-pass sessions, 0 searches spent — a graceful idle, not a stall.

The two productive windows are exactly the two sessions where the harvest breaker punched through. Between them the pool re-drained and the drought returned. Nothing here is code-fixable — the machine did the right thing (rest, don't grind) every dead pass. The cure is infra: rotate the egress IP so the harvest refills cleanly instead of on the throttle's whim.

Q3There's a finder_hard_wall fatal signature — is something actually broken?

No. It's a false alarm, and it's the one thing I fixed today. In the +18-parked noon session, two consecutive finder passes aborted on a transient Airtable 503 (SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE — "The service is temporarily unavailable"). The campaign driver treats two nonzero finder exits in a row as a hard wall, stopped the session, backed off — and the very next session wrote 180 channels. Airtable had a ~2-minute wobble; the loop self-healed exactly as designed.

The hourly check-in already knew this: its benignFinalized guard (added 07-29) skips the paid fix-agent when a hard-wall session still reaches [campaign] DONE — which is why it logged the stop as finder_hard_wall_benign and spent $0. But the debrief's fatal-signature scanner had never been taught the same carve-out (it only suppressed the No active terms drought case), so it cried wolf in the authoritative JSON. Now consistent: the debrief mirrors the check-in — a finalized hard wall is benign; only a genuine crash (which never reaches DONE) still escalates.
