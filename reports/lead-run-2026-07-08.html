The binding constraint

Email-verify — not scoring — is now the tightest gate. Only 54% of pitchable ≥6 leads had a findable, deliverable email. That single 46% loss (276 with no email on the channel, 143 with an invalid address) cost us more leads than the entire below-6 pile. To lift daily output, raising the verify rate matters as much as finding more ≥6 leads.

Why it matters

Phase 2 needed 2× the scanning to produce fewer parked leads. Two causes stacked: (1) the round-4 expansion into low-ticket trades (junk removal, towing, locksmith) drew creators with weaker booking intent and fewer published emails; (2) after ~5 runs the fresh-term reservoir was exhausted, so runs re-hit overlapping terms (new=0 on many). If the whole day had run at Phase 1 efficiency, ~500 parked would have taken ~1,550 scanned instead of 2,394.

Autocomplete mining verdict

Zero-quota YouTube-autocomplete mining (7,000+ suggestions across two rounds) was only useful for the “how to get clients for [business]” shape. Profession-descriptor seeds (“immigration attorney…”, “how to close…”) returned near-pure noise — pop-culture, geo, and exam-prep results (ace attorney, how to close a bag of chips). For legal/financial, hand-authored specialty lists beat mining decisively.

07

What worked well

  • Seed-then-prune before running. Pausing 585 weak terms and seeding proven veins lifted run-1 hit rate to 61% (vs 39% yesterday) and dropped demo-exclusion to 8%.
  • Host-finding is no longer a bottleneck. ~100% host-identified across every run — the fixed 5-step cascade held up under volume.
  • Measuring the verify rate mid-run instead of assuming it. The first verify batch confirmed 66%, which calibrated exactly how many more runs were needed rather than guessing.
  • Running verify concurrently with the finder. After Airtable stabilized, overlapping the two processes (different repos, different rows) saved substantial wall-clock without conflicts.
  • Real Estate over-delivered. An under-weighted vein going in, it became the #1 source of parked leads (112) — worth a bigger deliberate allocation next time.
08

Problems & hiccups

Airtable transient “Try again” errors

Aborted run 2 mid-pass and briefly blocked report queries. Writes are durable so no leads were lost, but it cost ~half a run. Airtable-side degradation, not our code — retry logic absorbed most of it.

Overlap collapse after ~5 runs

The fresh-term reservoir drained, so later runs re-hit the same terms (new=0 everywhere). Yield/run fell from 157 → ~38 score-≥6. Each late run needed mid-flight term authoring to stay alive.

Priority-ordering trap

New terms default to priority 50 — the same as ~100 stale Coaching terms — so they sat un-pulled until manually bumped. Fresh terms must outrank the backlog or they never run.

Trade-term dilution (round 4)

Expanding into low-ticket trades (junk removal, towing, locksmith) dropped hit rate to 40% and verify to 50%. These creators book less and publish fewer emails than professional-service coaches.

Verify-rate decay to 34%

As batches moved down-market and into overlap, deliverable-email rate slid from 66% to 34% — the clearest signal that we were scraping the bottom of the current term set.

Orchestrator JSONL logging gap

Only runs 1–5 wrote a yield line; runs 6–12 are missing from orchestrator-2026-07-08.jsonl. Reconstructable from finder logs, but worth fixing for clean run accounting.

09

How to run the next batch better

Ranked by expected impact on qualified-leads-per-hour. The theme: spend more of the day in the Phase-1 regime.

  1. Pre-build a 6–8 run reservoir of fresh professional terms before starting

    The single biggest lever. Have ~400–500 un-run terms queued in Legal specialties, “financial advisor for [profession]”, “how to get clients for [professional service]”, and RE-investor niches so the whole day runs at 30%+ efficiency and never falls into overlap re-runs. No mid-flight authoring.

  2. Weight toward firm-heavy niches to raise the verify rate

    Verify rate ≈ how firm-like the batch is. Legal, Financial-advisory, Tax, and RE-brokerages publish emails; personal-brand coaches and trades often don't. Shifting the mix toward firms could lift verify from 54% → 65%+, which is worth ~100 extra parked leads per day at the same scan volume.

  3. Cap or skip low-ticket trades

    “how to get clients for [junk removal / towing / locksmith]” diluted both hit and verify. Keep the shape for professional services (law, accounting, financial, medical practices, agencies); drop the sub-$X trades unless volume is desperate.

  4. Set new-term priority above the default automatically

    Bake the “bump fresh terms to 75” step into the add-terms flow so new terms always lead the queue. Manual bumping worked but is easy to forget and cost a run of buried terms.

  5. Budget by the real funnel constants

    Plan with ~1.8 pitchable ≥6 per desired parked lead (55% verify) and ~2.4 scanned per ≥6 (41% hit). For 500 parked: ~900 pitchable ≥6, ~2,200 scanned — achievable in ~5–6 runs if quality stays in the Phase-1 band.

  6. Stop autocomplete-mining legal/financial; keep it only for “get clients for [X]”

    Mining returned 95% noise for profession seeds. Maintain a curated, hand-authored master list of legal specialties and advisor-for-profession terms instead — it's higher yield per minute of effort.

  7. Overlap verify with the finder from the start

    It's safe (separate repos/rows) and saved real wall-clock once adopted. Kick off verify on each run's ≥6 batch as soon as the run lands, rather than pooling to the end.

  8. Investigate the 276 “no email found”

    60% of all losses. Worth a small spike: are we missing emails that exist (scraper coverage) vs. genuinely absent (channel has none)? If even 20% are recoverable with a deeper find pass, that's ~55 more parked leads/day for free.
