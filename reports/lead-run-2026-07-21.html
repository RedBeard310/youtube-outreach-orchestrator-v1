The three questions

Q1Why did parking come in at half of yesterday?

The frontier-discovery lane saturated. For 4 days (07-17 → 07-20) the autocomplete harvest was IP-blocked, and the whole pipeline was carried by the second supply lane: Claude inventing probe: veins (discover-veins.ts --frontier) that the finder mines in-session. That lane has now enumerated its own ground. Its 07-10 flagship veins visibly halved: Real Estate 159 → 45 and Health clinics 61 → 27 pitchable. Every discovery pass this cycle generated 40 candidates and every one deduped to already-known against the 6,983-term table — 0 unique in-ICP candidates, all 24 hours. Result: 207 pitchable of 737 discovered → +86 parked (2,677 → 2,763), verify holding ~50% — the drop is pure finding, not verification.

Q2Why did the finder hard-stop on every session?

Both independent term sources were dry at the same time — a first. The autocomplete harvest (proven-vein refuel) is still IP-blocked, so it correctly skipped 45 of 49 attempts. The frontier LLM (the only other source) produced 0 net-new. So the active pool drained to empty and never refilled: every finder run logged [anti-starvation] active pool exhausted and NO never-run paused terms remain → No active terms to process and aborted in 0.8s. Two consecutive aborts = "hard wall" → the session stops. That happened 44 / 44 sessions, finder fresh_pitchable summed to 0 across all 49 runs. This isn't the finder failing — it's the finder correctly refusing to burn quota on an empty term book.

Q3So is the pipeline broken? No — it self-healed. What actually changed?

Every guardrail built over the last 8 days held under a genuine dual-source wall: ① the 07-20 block-backoff (1d5d1f9) cut futile harvests into the block from 18 → 4 (only the bounded 6h self-clearing probes fired); ② the 07-14 discovery dry-guard suppressed 51 of 65 Claude-sonnet generations (only the 3h TTL re-probe fires); ③ the check-in classified all 47 hard-walls as finder_hard_wall_benign and never paged the fix-agent; ④ the loop rested ~30 min between sessions — no thrash. Net: $0 Claude spend, 0 crashes, 0 quota stops, no halt flag. The new fact of the day is simply that the invent-a-vein workaround has a floor, and we hit it. That's a supply problem, not a bug.
