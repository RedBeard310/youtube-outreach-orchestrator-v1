The three questions

Q1Why only +82 — a modest day?

A tale of two halves, and this time the good half came first. The PT morning/midday ran on freshly-harvested terms and produced most of the yield — roughly ~100 of 195 pitchable in 5 hours (peaks of 23, 21, 16). Then the pool drained and the evening went quiet: the PT evening and overnight were near-dead (16:00 PT onward mostly zeros; 20:00–23:00 PT completely dead).

The binding limiter was throughput, not efficiency. Per-pass yield was actually the best in a week (1.74 pitchable/pass vs 1.32 on 07-15, 1.69 on 07-14, 0.71 on 07-13) — the finder is excellent when it has terms. But it ran the fewest passes (112 vs 127 / 217 / 375) because it kept hitting the term wall and correctly resting: 20 of 24 sessions hard-stopped and backed off rather than grinding dead terms. The reservoir came back STOCK-UP on 20 of 24 pre-flights, but the harvest is cadence-gated to 4h, so it fired only 5 times (16 skipped) — every fade in between fell through to the LLM discover(), which yields ≈0 net-new against the saturated term table.

Q2Did it waste money, quota, or thrash?

No — this was the cleanest cycle yet. $0 Claude spend, 0 fatal signatures, 0 quota stops, YouTube quota never a factor. Every prior self-healing fix held: the discovery-dry guard suppressed wasted generations, and the hard-wall back-off + self-reload kept the run to 24 sessions with proper resting gaps — no 2.6-minute relaunch thrash. The hourly check-in fired no paid fix-agent all cycle.

And the fix shipped last cycle earned its keep: the new term_starvation heartbeat fired 7 times (18:42Z → 06:42Z) — precisely across the dead evening/overnight tail — and stayed $0 and out of the fix-agent path, exactly as designed. A dry finder is now visible same-hour instead of only at this debrief. The loss today was supply, not code: the machine behaved perfectly around a well it had emptied.

Q3What's the constraint — and what did we do about it?

The same governing input as every day since 07-09: term supply. This cycle isolated a specific failure mode — the between-harvest drain. The keyword harvest (our only refuel that produces real net-new terms) could previously fire only at a session's pre-flight. So once the early refill drained mid-window, every subsequent fade hit the dead discover() and the pool just bled out until the next session's pre-flight happened to align with the 4h gate. That's the dead evening tail, mechanically.

Fixed this cycle. The harvest now also fires on fade — the moment the active vein drains mid-session — at a shorter cadence floor (KEYWORD_HARVEST_MIN_INTERVAL_HOURS, default 2h vs the pre-flight 4h), because a fade is direct, measured evidence we're draining. It's additive and cadence-gated, so it can't over-fire or touch quota. Beyond that, the biggest lever is unchanged and unbuilt: the needs_contact recovery engine (now 3,079 parked creators, +98 this cycle) sidesteps the term-supply wall entirely. Deferred pending greenlight.
