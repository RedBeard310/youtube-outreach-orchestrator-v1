The three questions

Q1Why did parking crater to +7 — a 10× drop off yesterday's +67?

The backlog-drain lever finally ran out. For days 2–7 of the block, fresh finding was ~dead but the verify lane kept promoting the standing found-but-unverified pool — the last easily-emailable leads — into approved_hold (+151 → +86 → +52 → +67). That reservoir is now spent: per-session parked_gain was 5, 2, 3, 2 in the PT-morning cluster and then zero for every session after. needs_contact still grew (+17 → 3,683), i.e. the finder is still surfacing score-≥6 creators — they just have no verifiable email, so they park in the recovery lane, not the send lane.

Q2If the finder wrote 52 net-new channels, why did almost none convert?

Saturated-vein quality. Those 52 came from the keyword harvest squeezing through the rate-limit (~891 terms per re-probe) on veins we've mined for weeks — tax, superannuation, cross-border, buyers-agents. The ICP-prefilter kept only 3 of 891 candidates, and what the finder did surface verified at just ~26% (12 of 46 pitchable) versus the backlog's ~47%. This is a weak trickle off exhausted ground, not a recovery. The new fresh_finding_dead flag correctly reads false (52 ≥ 30 net-new) — its job is to say "finding isn't zero" — but +7 parked is the honest business number.

Q3Is the block lifting? The finder wrote channels again.

No — it's a rate-limit, not a clean endpoint. Today's harvest (04:47Z) served ~8 full seeds (≈2,000 requests) before the a–z expansion started returning HTTP 403; the circuit breaker tripped at 8 consecutive 403s and emitted AUTOCOMPLETE_ENDPOINT_BLOCKED. So the endpoint answers a burst, then throttles — which is why a term or two leaks through each 6-hour re-probe, but the pool can't actually refill. The block began 2026-07-17 (now ~6.9 days / day 8) and has not lifted. Remedy is unchanged and is infra: rotate the VPS egress IP / proxy.
