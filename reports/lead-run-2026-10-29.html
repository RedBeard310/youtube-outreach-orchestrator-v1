The three questions

Q1Was this the best reach day of the month, or not?

Not. It was the same starvation as every other day, wearing a recovery. 52 first touches · 36.1% against 19 and 3.8% over the prior seven days reads as the Whale re-offer queue finally letting new people through, which is the one lever the 10-15 note said to watch.

It was the 54 stranded people from yesterday's debrief. They were stamped sent_to_smartlead on 2026-10-13, the ICP audit paused their live sequences that afternoon, and so acceptance was credited while no email ever went out. The send path reverted the stamp inside one cycle and 50 of them flew, exactly the repair yesterday asked for, and the right outcome. But the re-push overwrites outreach_processed_at, so all 51 now carry a 10-28 stamp and nothing in the row says they are fifteen days old. Separating a drained backlog from new supply took a hand join of leads.siege_plan, where 50 of them sit under run_day 2026-10-13 already marked pushed.

The honest figure: 1 genuinely new person this cycle, and 5 across the eight days ending with it. A 36.1% day and a 3.8% week describe the same thing. The stranded pool is down to 4, so this cannot repeat at size.

Q2Yesterday's question was whether held mailboxes can recover. What happened?

The clean domain singled out as the honest test failed it within hours of carrying traffic. ytgrowthagency.com had 0 spam across 82 warmup emails yesterday and all three mailboxes at 96.7, held only by the 48-hour clock and the sick-sibling rule. All three returned to clear this cycle (08:05Z, 10:05Z, and one the evening before).

They were then handed the three largest loads in the entire push: 15, 15 and 14 emails against a 4-to-8 typical. By evening all three were stopped again on fresh warmup spam: 3 of 49 3 of 31 3 of 27, scores down to 65.9–69.7, and the provider cut volume 30% on all three.

The mechanism is in the slot arithmetic. A mailbox's slots are its daily allowance minus the follow-ups it already owes. A mailbox just back from a 48-hour suspension owes none, so it gets the biggest remainder in the fleet: a full load on its first day back, with no ramp. Four of five returners round-tripped inside the cycle, and band_held_but_healthy fell from 8 to 1, so capacity no longer comes back for free.

Q3Did anything actually produce this cycle?

Almost nothing, and the reason is a picked-over book rather than a fault. The recovery lane added 7 contact points · 2 addresses · 2 people against 94 and 21 over the prior week, and 1 lead parked. Its newest judged collect pass walked 150 leads and found 201 methods, 200 of them already on file, a full re-walk of lap 12 of a 2,392-lead book.

The binding cause is the ownership gate, not money: 134 Brave searches, 0 refusals, 103 candidate sites found, 97 refused by the gate because the page could not be tied to that creator. Brave credit buys more candidates for it to refuse. The largest error cause, 34-channel-page: page rendered with no outbound links at 45%, is that method correctly reporting creators with no links. The largest real fault is 17-podcast: iTunes at 28, which is the per-IP brake built on 10-15 giving up as designed, on a method that has not produced a point in 13 days.

Enrichment ran 2 batches, 2 leads, which is right: the shelf is 99.9% bundled with ten leads left to do.
